Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected outside the Lottery (i.e., NOT a Lottery pick) contains conditional Base Salary protection. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the scope of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection AND when that exception ends?
AA) The exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract, including any extended term; it ends only when the player's Contract is fully terminated.
BB) The exception applies only during the three initial Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract (not the option year), and ends definitively once the Contract is extended.
CC) The exception applies during all four Seasons (the three initial Seasons plus the Option Year) of the Rookie Scale Contract, but does NOT apply during the extended term of any Extension of that Contract; it ends definitively when the Contract is extended.
DD) The exception applies only during the first two Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract; it ends once the Team exercises the Fourth Year Option.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) states the Rookie Scale Contract exception applies 'with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract' and expressly 'shall not apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.' The four Seasons include the Option Year (fourth Season), so the exception covers all four Seasons of the base contract but terminates definitively upon extension.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick who was NOT selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the first Season is fully protected from the moment the Contract is signed.
BB) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract.
CC) The Base Salary for the first Season cannot contain Base Salary protection of any kind.
DD) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected only if the Team exercises the Fourth Year Option.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(ii) provides that for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by any First Round Pick other than a Lottery pick, the Base Salary for the first Season 'shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract.' This differs from a Lottery pick whose first Season is immediately and unconditionally fully protected.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly four (4) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible because the threshold is four (4) or more Years of Service.
BB) She is eligible because the CBA permits trade bonuses for any Veteran Player with at least three (3) Years of Service.
CC) She is ineligible because no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus.
DD) She is ineligible only if her Base Salary in the first Season exceeds the Standard Maximum Salary.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly states that 'No Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing such Contract or Extension, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain' a trade bonus. A player with exactly four (4) Years of Service has fewer than six (6) and is therefore ineligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays three (3) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and four (4) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays two (2) Seasons under her Contract (no extension). How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Nine (9) Seasons total (7 for Player A + 2 for Player B).
BB) Five (5) Seasons total (3 for Player A + 2 for Player B).
CC) Seven (7) Seasons total (3 for Player A under her original Contract + 4 under the Extension + 2 for Player B, but Extensions are excluded, so 3 + 2 = 5).
DD) Six (6) Seasons total (4 for Player A under the Extension + 2 for Player B).
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) states that only Seasons played 'while such player was so designated (but not including Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract)' count toward the maximum Core Player designation limit. For Player A, only the 3 Seasons under the original Contract (not the 4 Extension Seasons) count. For Player B, 2 Seasons count. Total = 3 + 2 = 5.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer definition)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly four (4) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary from the last Season of her prior Contract.
BB) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
CC) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
DD) The Maximum Player Salary pursuant to Art. V, Sec. 8(c) (a Core Qualifying Offer amount).
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' (subsection D) provides that for a player subject to a right of first refusal in accordance with Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer shall be 'the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.' A player with four (4) Years of Service who qualifies under Sec. 5(b) (four or more Years of Service) receives this salary floor.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) The Salary Cap for 2027 would have increased by more than 10% over the 2026 Salary Cap but for the cap on annual increases; the Shortfall equals the excess over a 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams.
BB) The Salary Cap for 2027 would have increased by more than 13% over the 2026 Salary Cap but for the cap on annual increases; the Shortfall equals the excess over a 13% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams.
CC) Total Salaries for the 2027 Salary Cap Year are less than the Salary Cap multiplied by the total number of Teams; the Shortfall equals that difference.
DD) The Salary Cap for 2027 decreases by more than 13% from the 2026 Salary Cap; the Shortfall equals the amount of the decrease beyond 13% multiplied by the total number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap 'would have increased by more than ten percent (10%)... (or, with respect to the Salary Cap for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, by more than thirteen percent (13%) from the Salary Cap for the 2026 Salary Cap Year) but for the operation of Section 1(a)(ii).' The Shortfall equals the amount in excess of the applicable cap (13% for 2027) multiplied by the total number of Teams.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)(i)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the in-network annual deductible per covered individual AND the percentage of eligible charges covered after the deductible is met but BEFORE the out-of-pocket threshold is reached?
AA) $600 in-network annual deductible per individual; 70% coverage after the deductible until the out-of-pocket threshold.
BB) $2,500 in-network annual deductible per individual; 80% coverage after the deductible until the out-of-pocket threshold.
CC) $600 in-network annual deductible per individual; 80% coverage after the deductible until the covered individual has paid $3,000 per person.
DD) $1,800 in-network annual deductible per individual (family rate applied per person); 60% coverage after the deductible.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) specifies that in-network services include 'a $600 annual deductible per covered individual ($1,800 per family), and coverage at eighty percent (80%) until the covered individual has paid $3,000 per person ($9,000 per family) in a calendar year, and one hundred percent (100%) thereafter.' The $600 is the individual (not family) deductible and 80% applies after the deductible until the $3,000 out-of-pocket threshold.
Q8Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii); Art. I Sec. (aaa)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2033 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
DD) The CBA does not define a Designated 20% Share of SBR formula for the 2033 Salary Cap Year because 2033 falls outside the term of the Agreement.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) defines the Designated 20% Share of SBR only for Salary Cap Years 2027 through 2032 (with 2031 and 2032 sharing the same formula). Art. I, Sec. (aaa) confirms the final Salary Cap Year under the Agreement is the 2032 Salary Cap Year, ending December 31, 2032. The 2033 Salary Cap Year is not covered by this Agreement and has no defined formula.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)(iii)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, a non-Developmental Player whose Base Salary is exactly $500,000 is entitled to Team-provided housing (a one-bedroom apartment) in the home city of her WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a)(iii) provides that for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, all non-Developmental Players 'whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000 during the corresponding Season will be provided with a one-bedroom apartment.' A player with a Base Salary of exactly $500,000 is within the 'less than or equal to $500,000' threshold and is therefore entitled to Team-provided housing.
Q10Art. VIII Sec. (c)T/F
True or False: Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by the same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. VIII, Sec. (c) explicitly distinguishes between the two adjustment mechanisms: First Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are increased (or decreased) by the percentage change in the Salary Cap, while Second Round and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are 'increased by 4%' (a flat 4% increase, not tied to the Salary Cap change). These are two separate and distinct adjustment mechanisms.
