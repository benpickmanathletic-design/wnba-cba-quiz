Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its THIRD Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception does not apply because the player is a non-Lottery pick; non-Lottery Rookie Scale Contracts always count against the seven-contract limit.
BB) The exception applies and the Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts containing Base Salary protection, because the Contract is still within its initial four Seasons and has not been extended.
CC) The exception applies only for the first two Seasons of a non-Lottery First Round Pick's Rookie Scale Contract; by the third Season it counts against the limit.
DD) The exception does not apply because Base Salary protection for Seasons three and four only vests upon exercise of the fourth-year option, meaning there is no Base Salary protection to count in Season three.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) provides that any Rookie Scale Contract containing Base Salary protection shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection, and this exception applies with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract (but not during any extended term). Because the Contract is in its third Season and has not been extended, the exception still applies.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the first Season is fully protected from the date the Contract is signed, with no conditions.
BB) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract.
CC) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
DD) A non-Lottery First Round Pick's Rookie Scale Contract may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind for the first Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by any First Round Pick other than a Lottery pick, the Base Salary for the first Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract. This distinguishes non-Lottery picks (conditional protection tied to the first Regular Season) from Lottery picks (unconditional full protection for Season 1).
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly six (6) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is ineligible to include a trade bonus because only players with more than six (6) Years of Service may do so.
BB) She is eligible to include a trade bonus because Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) requires at least six (6) Years of Service, and she has exactly six (6).
CC) She is ineligible because the trade bonus provision only applies to players signing Extensions, not new Contracts.
DD) She is eligible, but the trade bonus is capped at five percent (5%) of remaining Base Salary rather than ten percent (10%) since she has exactly six (6) Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) provides that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly six (6) Years of Service meets this threshold and is therefore eligible to include a trade bonus in her new Contract.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays two (2) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and one (1) Season under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays four (4) Seasons under her Contract and two (2) Seasons under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Nine (9) Seasons total: three for Player A (2 + 1 extension) and six for Player B (4 + 2 extension).
BB) Six (6) Seasons total: two for Player A (extensions excluded) and four for Player B (extensions excluded).
CC) Eight (8) Seasons total: two for Player A and six for Player B.
DD) Five (5) Seasons total: two for Player A and three for Player B (only Seasons up to the CBA maximum are counted).
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) specifies that only the Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the Team's maximum designation limit — Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract are explicitly excluded. Therefore, Player A contributes 2 Seasons and Player B contributes 4 Seasons, for a total of 6.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (definition of Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly six (6) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary from the last Season of her prior Contract.
BB) The applicable Minimum Player Salary for a player with 1 to 3 Years of Service.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' provides that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) (i.e., a Veteran Free Agent with four or more Years of Service), a Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. A player with six (6) Years of Service qualifies under this provision.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) The Shortfall is triggered if the Salary Cap for 2028 would have increased by more than thirteen percent (13%) from the 2027 Salary Cap but for the cap on increases; computed as the excess over 13% multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) The Shortfall is triggered if the Salary Cap for 2028 would have increased by more than ten percent (10%) from the 2027 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); computed as the additional amount in excess of a ten percent (10%) increase multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2028 Season.
CC) The Shortfall is triggered if Total Salaries for 2028 fall below the Salary Cap for 2028 multiplied by the number of Teams; computed as the difference between those two figures.
DD) The Shortfall is triggered if the Salary Cap for 2028 would have decreased by more than ten percent (10%) from the 2027 Salary Cap; computed as the amount of the excess decrease multiplied by the number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall is triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than ten percent (10%) of the prior Salary Cap Year's Salary Cap (or 13% for 2027 specifically) but for the cap limitation. For the 2028 Salary Cap Year, the applicable threshold is ten percent (10%), and the Shortfall equals the excess amount above that ten percent increase multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2028 Season.

Q8Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary exceeds $500,000 are entitled to Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing in their WNBA Team's home city.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) provides housing entitlement for all non-Developmental Players in the 2026, 2027, and 2028 Seasons, and for non-Developmental Players with Base Salary at or below $500,000 in the 2029 and 2030 Seasons. No Team-provided housing obligation is established for any Season after 2030, so non-Developmental Players — at any salary level — are not entitled to Team-provided housing in the 2031 or 2032 Seasons.
Q9Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses eight (8) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her 2.5% of Base Salary per game for all eight games (totaling 20% of Base Salary). The WNBA then seeks to impose an additional fine of 3% of Base Salary for the same eight missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the permissibility of the WNBA's additional fine?
AA) The WNBA's additional fine is permissible because the Team's fine does not preclude the WNBA from also fining the player, provided the WNBA fine does not independently exceed 20% of Base Salary.
BB) The WNBA's additional fine is impermissible because Art. XIV, Sec. 7 states that if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine for missed Regular Season games, neither the Team nor the WNBA shall be permitted to impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games.
CC) The WNBA's additional fine is permissible because the 20% cap applies only to fines imposed by the Team, not to combined WNBA and Team fines.
DD) The WNBA's additional fine is permissible because the Team's fine did not exceed 2.5% per game; the 20% aggregate cap is a separate ceiling that applies only to the WNBA's fines.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 explicitly states that if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine pursuant to that Section in respect of missed Regular Season games, neither the Team nor the WNBA shall be permitted to impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games. Having reached the 20% aggregate cap, the WNBA's additional 3% fine for the same games is impermissible.
Q10Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2030 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) specifies the Designated 20% Share of SBR for the 2030 Salary Cap Year as 30.5% of League Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year (i.e., the 2029 Salary Cap Year) plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year. This is distinct from the 2029 formula (12.25%) and the 2031/2032 formula (13%).
