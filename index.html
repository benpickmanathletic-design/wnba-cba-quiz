Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its SECOND Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception does not apply because non-Lottery First Round Picks are excluded from the Rookie Scale Contract exception entirely.
BB) The exception applies during the second Season because it covers all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and the Contract has not been extended.
CC) The exception no longer applies after the first Season for non-Lottery First Round Picks, regardless of extension status.
DD) The exception applies only if the player's Base Salary was renegotiated pursuant to Art. VII, Sec. 5(c)(ii).
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), any Rookie Scale Contract that contains Base Salary protection shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection; this exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract (regardless of pick type) and ends only upon extension. Since the Contract has not been extended and is only in its second Season, the exception remains active.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick (a First Round Pick NOT selected in the Lottery), which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the SECOND Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the second Season is fully protected from the moment the Contract is signed.
BB) The Base Salary for the second Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
CC) The Base Salary for the second Season shall become fully protected only if the Team exercises the fourth-year option.
DD) No Base Salary protection of any kind is permitted for the second Season of such a Contract.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(ii), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by any First Round Pick other than a Lottery pick, the Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by the Contract. This mirrors the same trigger used for the first Season but applied to the second Season.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly three (3) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible because three (3) Years of Service exceeds the two-year minimum threshold for trade bonuses.
BB) She is eligible because the CBA imposes no minimum Years of Service requirement for trade bonuses in new Contracts.
CC) She is not eligible because a player must have at least six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing to include a trade bonus.
DD) She is eligible, but the trade bonus may not exceed five percent (5%) of remaining Base Salary due to her limited service time.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly three (3) Years of Service does not meet this threshold and is therefore ineligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays five (5) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and two (2) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays two (2) Seasons under her Contract and three (3) Seasons under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Twelve (12) Seasons total: seven (7) for Player A and five (5) for Player B.
BB) Five (5) Seasons total: only the non-extended Seasons for Player A count, and Player B's designation is the Team's current one so it is not yet counted.
CC) Seven (7) Seasons total: five (5) for Player A (Contract only, not Extension) and two (2) for Player B (Contract only, not Extension).
DD) Ten (10) Seasons total: five (5) for Player A and five (5) for Player B, as Extensions are excluded entirely from the count.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), only Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the Team's maximum—Seasons covered by any Extension are expressly excluded. Player A contributes five (5) Seasons (Contract only) and Player B contributes two (2) Seasons (Contract only), for a total of seven (7).
Q5Art. I (definition of Qualifying Offer); Art. VI Sec. 5(b)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly nine (9) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of her fourth-year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary applicable to the Season covered by the Qualifying Offer.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Core Qualifying Offer amount equal to the Maximum Player Salary under Art. V, Sec. 8(c).
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subsection (D), a Restricted Qualifying Offer for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. Years of Service beyond four (4) do not change this floor formula for a Restricted Qualifying Offer.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% but for the cap; Shortfall equals the excess above 13% multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% but for the operation of Art. VII, Sec. 1(a)(ii); Shortfall equals the additional amount above a 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2029 Season.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries fall below the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams; Shortfall equals that deficit amount.
DD) Triggered when the Designated 20% Share of SBR exceeds Salary Cap Benefits by more than 10%; Shortfall equals that excess multiplied by the number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap for a given year (other than 2027, which uses a 13% threshold) would have increased by more than ten percent (10%) but for the operation of the cap growth limit in Sec. 1(a)(ii). The Shortfall equals the amount in excess of the 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams participating in the WNBA in that Season. The 13% threshold applies only to the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)(i)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states BOTH the out-of-network annual deductible per covered individual AND the out-of-network family deductible?
AA) $2,500 per individual and $5,000 per family.
BB) $600 per individual and $1,800 per family.
CC) $2,500 per individual and $6,250 per family.
DD) $1,800 per individual and $6,250 per family.
CBA Reference
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i), for non-Toronto Tempo players, out-of-network services carry a $2,500 annual deductible per covered individual and a $6,250 per family deductible. The in-network deductibles ($600 individual / $1,800 family) are distinct and lower.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses nine (9) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her 2.5% of Base Salary per game for all nine (9) games, totaling 22.5% of Base Salary. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the application of the aggregate cap?
AA) The full 22.5% fine is permissible because the 20% cap applies only to playoff games.
BB) The aggregate fines are capped at 20% of Base Salary for Regular Season games in any Season, so only 20% may be collected, and the 2.5% excess must be returned or forgiven.
CC) The 20% aggregate cap does not apply because the Team may impose up to 2.5% per game with no overall ceiling.
DD) The 22.5% is permissible only if the WNBA, not the Team, imposes the fines.
CBA Reference
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, in no event may aggregate fines imposed pursuant to that Section exceed twenty percent (20%) of the player's Base Salary for missing Regular Season games in any Season. Nine games at 2.5% each yields 22.5%, which exceeds the 20% cap; therefore only 20% may be imposed, and the remaining 2.5% cannot be collected as a fine (though a separate suspension could be imposed without implicating the fine cap).
Q9Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2031 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), for each of the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years, the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals 30.5% of League Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year. For 2031, the preceding year is 2030, making option C correct.
Q10Art. VIII Sec. (c)T/F
True or False: Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by the same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), First Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by the percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap from year to year, while Second Round and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are instead increased by a fixed four percent (4%) each Season regardless of Salary Cap movement. These are two distinct adjustment mechanisms.
