Q1Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly seven (7) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is ineligible because a trade bonus requires at least eight (8) Years of Service.
BB) She is eligible because she has at least six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing.
CC) She is ineligible because the trade bonus provision only applies to Extensions, not new Contracts.
DD) She is eligible only if the Contract is also signed in connection with an agreement to trade the Contract under Art. V, Sec. 3(f).
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly provides that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with seven (7) Years of Service meets the threshold and is therefore eligible.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick who was NOT selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the THIRD and FOURTH Seasons of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the third Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested prior to the first day of the third Regular Season; the fourth Season is fully protected upon the Team's exercise of the fourth-year option.
BB) The Base Salary for both the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected if the option for the fourth Season is exercised by the Team.
CC) The Base Salary for the third Season is fully protected from signing; the fourth Season becomes fully protected only if the option is exercised.
DD) Neither the third nor the fourth Season can contain any Base Salary protection for a non-Lottery First Round Pick.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(ii) states that for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by any First Round Pick not selected in the Lottery, the Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons shall become fully protected if the option for the fourth Season is exercised by the Team. This mirrors the Lottery pick rule for Seasons 3 and 4.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract that has been EXTENDED: which of the following MOST ACCURATELY states whether the extension term benefits from the exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception continues to apply for all four Seasons of the original Rookie Scale Contract term, including any extended term.
BB) The exception applies only during the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and expressly does NOT apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.
CC) The exception ceases the moment the Extension is signed, even if the original four Seasons have not yet been played.
DD) The exception applies to the extended term only if the Base Salary was renegotiated pursuant to Art. VII, Sec. 5(c)(ii).
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) states that the Rookie Scale Contract exception only applies with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract, and expressly shall not apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended. The exception definitively ceases as to any extended term.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays two (2) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and four (4) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays four (4) Seasons under her Contract (no extension). How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 10 Seasons (2 + 4 + 4).
BB) 6 Seasons (2 + 4), because Extension Seasons are excluded.
CC) 8 Seasons (2 + 4 + 2), because only the first two Extension Seasons count.
DD) 2 Seasons (Player A's Contract Seasons only), because Player B's designation resets the count.
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) provides that only Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player — but NOT Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract — count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit. For Player A, only the 2 non-extended Seasons count; for Player B, all 4 Contract Seasons count, for a total of 6.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly eight (8) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's fourth-year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Supermax Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subsection (D), a Restricted Qualifying Offer for a player subject to a right of first refusal in accordance with Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. Years of Service beyond four (4) do not change this floor.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% from the 2028 cap but for the cap-growth ceiling; computed as the excess amount multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2029 Season.
BB) Triggered when Total Salaries leaguewide fall below the aggregate Salary Cap; computed as that aggregate shortfall.
CC) Triggered when any Team's Team Salary falls below the Minimum Team Salary; computed as the sum of each Team's individual shortfall.
DD) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% from the 2028 cap; computed as the excess multiplied by the number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the cap would have increased by more than 10% (the 13% limit applies only to the 2027 cap year) but for the ceiling in Sec. 1(a)(ii). The Shortfall equals the additional amount by which the cap would have increased beyond 10%, multiplied by the total number of Teams for that Season.
Q7Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2032 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) provides that for each of the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years, the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals 30.5% of League Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year. For 2032, the preceding year is 2031.
Q8Art. VIII Sec. (c)4-choice
Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons AFTER 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by which mechanism?
AA) The same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
BB) A flat 4% increase per Season regardless of any change in the Salary Cap.
CC) No adjustment; Second and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are fixed for the entire CBA term.
DD) An increase equal to the Consumer Price Index adjustment used for meal expense allowances.
CBA Reference
Art. VIII, Sec. (c) distinguishes between First Round Picks and all others: First Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by the percentage change in the Salary Cap, whereas Rookie Scale Amounts for Second and Third Round Picks (and 'All Others') are increased by a flat 4% per Season regardless of cap movement.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, all non-Developmental Players regardless of Base Salary level are entitled to a Team-provided one-bedroom apartment in the home city of their WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) expressly limits Team-provided home-city housing to specific Seasons and salary thresholds. The provision for all non-Developmental Players applies only through the 2028 Season; for 2029 and 2030 it is capped at players with Base Salary ≤ $500,000; no Team-provided home-city housing provision is extended to 2031 or 2032 for any salary tier.
Q10Art. XIV Sec. 7T/F
True or False: Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine pursuant to the 2.5%-per-game provision for missed Regular Season games, the 20% aggregate Regular Season fine cap expressly DOES also limit any salary reduction arising from a suspension imposed on the player for those same missed Regular Season games.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that nothing in Section 7 shall be construed to limit a Team's right to impose a suspension for the same (or other) missed games, and that 'the 20% and 25% caps noted above shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension.' The caps govern fines only, not suspension-related salary reductions.
