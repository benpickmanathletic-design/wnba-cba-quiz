Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its SECOND Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The Rookie Scale Contract counts against the seven-contract limit because the second season Base Salary has already become fully protected.
BB) The Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the seven-contract limit, because the exception applies during all four Seasons of the original Rookie Scale Contract term (not any extended term), regardless of whether protection has been triggered.
CC) The Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the seven-contract limit only if the Team has not yet requested waivers, meaning protection has not attached for Season 2.
DD) The Rookie Scale Contract counts against the seven-contract limit only after the option for the fourth Season has been exercised.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), any Rookie Scale Contract that contains Base Salary protection shall NOT count against the per-team limit of seven Player Contracts with Base Salary protection, and this exception applies during the four Seasons of the original Rookie Scale Contract term regardless of when protection attaches. The exception explicitly does not apply to any extended term.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the SECOND Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the second Season is fully protected from the moment the Contract is signed.
BB) The Base Salary for the second Season shall become fully protected if the option for the fourth Season is exercised by the Team.
CC) The Base Salary for the second Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
DD) No Base Salary protection of any kind may be provided for the second Season of such a Contract.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(iii), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, the Base Salary for the second Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract. The first Season carries no protection, and the third/fourth Seasons are tied to the option exercise.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly five (5) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible to include a trade bonus because she has at least four (4) Years of Service.
BB) She is not eligible to include a trade bonus because the minimum threshold is six (6) Years of Service.
CC) She is eligible to include a trade bonus only if the Contract is a multi-year deal.
DD) She is not eligible to include a trade bonus because only players with seven (7) or more Years of Service may do so.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly five (5) Years of Service falls below this threshold and is therefore ineligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays four (4) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and two (2) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays two (2) Seasons under her Contract and one (1) Season under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Nine (9) Seasons total: six for Player A and three for Player B.
BB) Six (6) Seasons total: four for Player A (Extension Seasons excluded) and two for Player B (Extension Season excluded).
CC) Seven (7) Seasons total: four for Player A plus two for Player A's Extension plus one for Player B (Extension excluded).
DD) Five (5) Seasons total: four for Player A and one for Player B (both Extension terms excluded).
CBA Reference
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), only Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the maximum — Seasons covered by any Extension to that Contract are explicitly excluded. Therefore, four Seasons for Player A and two Seasons for Player B count, totaling six (6).
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly twelve (12) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary in the last Season of her prior Contract.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Supermax Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' subsection (D), a Restricted Qualifying Offer for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) must be the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. Years of Service beyond the threshold in Sec. 5(b) do not alter this floor formula.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% from the 2030 cap but for the cap; Shortfall equals the excess above 13% multiplied by total Teams.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% from the 2030 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); Shortfall equals the excess above a 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2031 Season.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries league-wide fall below the Salary Cap multiplied by the total number of Teams; Shortfall equals that deficit.
DD) Triggered when any individual Team's Team Salary falls below the Minimum Team Salary; Shortfall equals 85% of the Salary Cap for that Team.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises only when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% (the 13% exception applies only to the 2027 cap year) but for the cap imposed by Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the amount in excess of the 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams in the applicable Season.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states BOTH the in-network family deductible AND the in-network family out-of-pocket threshold at which in-network coverage reaches 100%?
AA) $1,800 family deductible; $9,000 family out-of-pocket threshold.
BB) $6,250 family deductible; $20,000 family out-of-pocket threshold.
CC) $1,800 family deductible; $6,250 family out-of-pocket threshold.
DD) $600 family deductible; $3,000 family out-of-pocket threshold.
CBA Reference
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i), for non-Toronto Tempo players the in-network plan provides a $600 annual deductible per covered individual ($1,800 per family), and coverage reaches 100% once the covered individual has paid $3,000 per person ($9,000 per family) in a calendar year. The $6,250 and $20,000 figures apply to out-of-network coverage.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 7T/F
True or False: Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, if the WNBA imposes fines totaling exactly 20% of a player's Base Salary for missing Regular Season games, the Team is then prohibited from imposing any additional fine or suspension on the player for those same missed games.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, the 20% aggregate cap applies only to fines — it does not limit a Team's right to impose a suspension or other discipline for the same missed games. The CBA explicitly states that the 20% and 25% caps 'shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension,' and further that neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose an additional fine (not a suspension) for the same missed games once the fine has been imposed.
Q9Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2029 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), for the 2029 Salary Cap Year the Designated 20% Share of SBR is defined as 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year. The 12.5% Team Revenue percentage applies to 2030, not 2029.
Q10Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, ALL non-Developmental Players — regardless of Base Salary level — are entitled to Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing in their WNBA Team's home city.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a)(iii), for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, only non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000 during the corresponding Season are provided with a Team-provided one-bedroom apartment. Players with Base Salaries exceeding $500,000 in those Seasons are not entitled to this benefit under that provision.
