Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its FOURTH Season (not extended). Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception still applies because the fourth Season is the Option Year and remains part of the Rookie Scale Contract's initial four-Season scope.
BB) The exception no longer applies because the fourth Season is an extended term under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii).
CC) The exception still applies because the fourth Season is within the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and the Contract has not been extended.
DD) The exception never applied to non-Lottery First Round Picks at any point during the Rookie Scale Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) states the exception applies 'with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract' and explicitly does NOT apply 'with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.' Since the Contract has not been extended, the fourth Season (Option Year) still falls within the four-Season window and the exception continues to apply.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract.
BB) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected automatically upon signing of the Rookie Scale Contract.
CC) Such a Rookie Scale Contract may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind for the first Season.
DD) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected only if the Team exercises the fourth-Season option.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(iii) explicitly states: 'For clarity, such a Rookie Scale Contract may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind for the first Season of such Contract.' This is a distinct and stricter rule than that applying to First Round Picks of any type.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly one (1) Year of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible to include a trade bonus because she has at least one Year of Service.
BB) She is ineligible to include a trade bonus because she has fewer than six (6) Years of Service.
CC) She is eligible to include a trade bonus only if the Contract has a term of more than two Seasons.
DD) She is eligible to include a trade bonus only with the WNBA's prior written approval.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) provides that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. With only one Year of Service, this player is clearly ineligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays four (4) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and one (1) Season under an Extension. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays three (3) Seasons under her Contract and zero (0) Seasons under any Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 8 Seasons (4 + 1 + 3 + 0).
BB) 7 Seasons (4 + 3), because Extension Seasons are excluded from the Core Player season count.
CC) 5 Seasons (4 + 1), because only Player A's Seasons count, as Player B's designation is invalidated.
DD) 9 Seasons because all Seasons under any Contract or Extension are counted.
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) specifies that only Seasons played 'pursuant to a Contract signed while such player was so designated (but not including Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract)' count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit. Therefore, Player A contributes 4 Seasons (not the 1 Extension Season) and Player B contributes 3 Seasons, for a total of 7.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer definition)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly ten (10) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary in the last Season of her prior Contract.
BB) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
CC) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
DD) The Minimum Annual Salary for a player with 1-3 Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' clause (D) provides that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), a Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal 'the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.' This applies regardless of whether the player has 5, 10, or any other number of Years of Service, so long as she qualifies under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% from the 2029 Salary Cap; the Shortfall equals the excess above the 13% cap multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% from the 2029 Salary Cap; the Shortfall equals the excess above the 10% cap multiplied by the number of Teams.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries fall below the aggregate Salary Cap; the Shortfall equals the difference between aggregate Team Salaries and the aggregate Salary Cap.
DD) Triggered when any Team's Team Salary falls below the Minimum Team Salary; the Shortfall equals 85% of the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of non-compliant Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% (applicable for all Salary Cap Years after 2027) but for the operation of the cap-on-cap provision in Sec. 1(a)(ii). The Shortfall equals the additional amount by which the Salary Cap would have increased beyond the 10% limit, multiplied by the total number of Teams participating that Season. The 13% threshold applies only to the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)(i)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the out-of-network coverage percentage for most eligible charges AFTER the deductible is met and BEFORE the out-of-pocket threshold is reached, AND the per-person out-of-pocket threshold at which out-of-network coverage reaches 100%?
AA) 70% coverage for most eligible charges; $8,000 per person out-of-pocket threshold.
BB) 80% coverage for most eligible charges; $3,000 per person out-of-pocket threshold.
CC) 60% coverage for most eligible charges; $8,000 per person out-of-pocket threshold.
DD) 60% coverage for most eligible charges; $20,000 per person out-of-pocket threshold.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) states that for out-of-network services, coverage is at 60% for most eligible charges (70% for certain other eligible charges) until the covered individual has paid $8,000 per person in a calendar year, after which coverage reaches 100%. The $20,000 figure is the family out-of-pocket threshold, not the per-person threshold.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses six (6) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her the maximum 2.5% of Base Salary per game for all six games (totaling 15% of Base Salary). The WNBA then separately seeks to impose a suspension for those same six missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the permissibility of the suspension and the application of the 20% fine cap?
AA) The WNBA is barred from imposing a suspension for the same missed games because the Team already imposed a fine, and the 20% cap prevents any additional salary reduction.
BB) The WNBA may impose a suspension for the same missed games; the 20% aggregate fine cap does not apply to salary reductions resulting from suspensions, so lost salary from the suspension is not capped at 20%.
CC) The WNBA may impose a suspension, but combined salary reduction from the fine and suspension together cannot exceed 20% of Base Salary.
DD) The Team must first rescind the 15% fine before the WNBA can impose a suspension for the same games.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 explicitly states that 'nothing in this Section 7 shall be construed to limit a Team's right...to impose a suspension or other discipline on a player for the same (or other) missed games' and that 'the 20% and 25% caps noted above shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension.' The bar on additional fines (not suspensions) by the other entity for the same missed games applies only to fines, not to suspensions.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, all non-Developmental Players — regardless of their Base Salary — are explicitly guaranteed Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing in the home city of their WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) guarantees one-bedroom apartments for all non-Developmental Players only for the 2026, 2027, and 2028 Seasons, and then only for players with Base Salary ≤$500,000 for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons. The CBA contains no explicit provision extending Team-provided housing to the 2031 or 2032 Seasons for any non-Developmental Players, regardless of salary.
Q10Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2027 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
