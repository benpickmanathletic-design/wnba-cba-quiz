Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its FOURTH Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception still applies because the Contract has not been extended and the fourth Season is within the initial four-Season window.
BB) The exception no longer applies because the fourth Season of a Lottery Pick's Rookie Scale Contract is an Option Year that is treated as an extension for purposes of this rule.
CC) The exception no longer applies because the CBA's exception is limited to the first three Seasons of any Rookie Scale Contract, regardless of pick type.
DD) The exception applies only if the Team has not yet used all seven slots for protected contracts.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the seven-contract Base Salary protection limit applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract itself, but ceases to apply once the Contract is extended. Because this Lottery Pick's Contract is in its fourth Season and has not been extended, the exception still applies.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)(i)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the SECOND Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the second Season is fully protected from the moment the Contract is signed.
BB) The Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
CC) The Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected only if the Team exercises the option for the fourth Season.
DD) The second Season Base Salary for a Lottery Pick has no protection of any kind unless separately negotiated.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i) states that for a Lottery First Round Pick, the Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract. This is distinct from the first Season, which is immediately fully protected.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly seven (7) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible because the trade bonus provision requires only five (5) Years of Service, and seven (7) exceeds that threshold.
BB) She is ineligible because the trade bonus requires at least eight (8) Years of Service.
CC) She is ineligible because a player must have fewer than six (6) Years of Service to include a trade bonus.
DD) She is eligible only if the Contract is an Extension, not a new Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) provides that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with seven (7) Years of Service meets this threshold and is therefore eligible to include a trade bonus.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays three (3) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and four (4) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays five (5) Seasons under her Contract and zero (0) Seasons under any Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 12 Seasons total (7 for Player A + 5 for Player B).
BB) 8 Seasons total (3 for Player A + 5 for Player B), because only Seasons under the original Contract while designated count.
CC) 15 Seasons total (all Seasons for both players count).
DD) 5 Seasons total (only Player B's Seasons count because Player A's Contract was extended).
CBA Reference
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), only Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the Team's limit — Seasons under any Extension of that Contract do not count. Therefore, Player A contributes 3 Seasons and Player B contributes 5 Seasons, for a total of 8.
Q5Art. I (Qualifying Offer definition); Art. VI Sec. 5(b)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly eight (8) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) The Standard Maximum Salary applicable to her Years of Service tier.
BB) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
CC) 105% of her fourth year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
DD) The Maximum Player Salary pursuant to Art. V, Sec. 8(c).
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' subsection (D) states that for a player subject to a right of first refusal in accordance with Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer must be the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This applies to Veteran Free Agents like one with eight (8) Years of Service.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2033 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% from the prior year but for the cap on increases; Shortfall equals the excess amount above a 13% increase multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% from the prior Salary Cap Year but for Art. VII, Sec. 1(a)(ii); Shortfall equals the additional amount by which the Cap would have increased (i.e., the amount in excess of a 10% increase) multiplied by the total number of Teams that Season.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries are less than the aggregate Salary Cap across all Teams; Shortfall equals the difference.
DD) Triggered when any individual Team Salary falls below the Minimum Team Salary; Shortfall equals 85% of the Salary Cap.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall is triggered when the Cap would have risen by more than the applicable percentage limit (13% for 2027, 10% for all subsequent years) but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii). The 2033 Salary Cap Year would be subject to the 10% cap, and the Shortfall equals the excess above that 10% increase multiplied by the total number of participating Teams.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)(i)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states BOTH the in-network per-person out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100% AND the out-of-network per-person out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) In-network: $3,000 per person; Out-of-network: $8,000 per person.
BB) In-network: $9,000 per person; Out-of-network: $20,000 per person.
CC) In-network: $3,000 per person; Out-of-network: $6,250 per family.
DD) In-network: $1,800 per family; Out-of-network: $8,000 per person.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) specifies that in-network coverage reaches 100% after the covered individual pays $3,000 per person in a calendar year, and out-of-network coverage reaches 100% after the covered individual pays $8,000 per person in a calendar year. The $9,000 and $20,000 figures are the corresponding family thresholds.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses five (5) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her the maximum 2.5% of Base Salary per game for all five games (totaling 12.5% of Base Salary). The WNBA then separately seeks to fine the player an additional 8% of Base Salary for the same five missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the permissibility of the WNBA's additional fine?
AA) Permissible in full, because the 20% aggregate cap has not yet been reached and the WNBA may always add fines on top of Team fines.
BB) Impermissible in any amount, because Art. XIV, Sec. 7 provides that once the Team imposes a fine pursuant to Sec. 7 for missed games, neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose an additional fine for the same missed games.
CC) Permissible only up to 7.5% (to reach the 20% aggregate cap), because the WNBA may top up a Team fine to reach the cap.
DD) Permissible in full because the WNBA's fine is independent of the Team's fine and a separate 20% cap applies to each.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 explicitly states that if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine pursuant to Sec. 7 in respect of missed Regular Season games, 'neither the Team nor the WNBA, as the case may be, shall be permitted to impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games.' Because the Team already imposed fines for these games, the WNBA's additional fine for the same games is impermissible.
Q9Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2033 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 20% of Shared Basketball Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, with no separate League and Team Revenue split.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) specifies that for 'each of the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years,' the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals 30.5% of League Revenue plus 13% of Team Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year. The 2033 Salary Cap Year follows the same formula as 2031 and 2032 (30.5% League Revenue + 13% Team Revenue from the prior year), since no different formula is specified for 2033.
Q10Art. VIII Sec. (c)T/F
True or False: Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by the same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. VIII, Sec. (c) provides that Rookie Scale Amounts for First Round Picks are adjusted by the percentage change in the Salary Cap, but for Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks, the Rookie Scale Amounts are instead increased by a flat 4% per Season — not by the Salary Cap percentage change. These are two distinct adjustment mechanisms.
