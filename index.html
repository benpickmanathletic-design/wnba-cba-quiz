Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery' is currently in its SECOND Season (not extended). Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection at this point in time?
AA. The exception does not apply because Lottery picks are subject to the standard seven-contract limit.
BB. The exception applies during the second Season because the Contract remains within its four-Season original term and has not been extended.
CC. The exception applies only during the first Season of such Contract and expires automatically at the end of that Season.
DD. The exception applies only after the option for the fourth Season has been exercised by the Team.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), any Rookie Scale Contract containing Base Salary protection shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection; this exception applies 'with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract' and explicitly does not apply to the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended. Since the Contract is in its second Season and has not been extended, the exception remains in full force.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the SECOND Season of such Contract?
AA. The Base Salary for the second Season is fully protected from the date of signing with no conditions.
BB. The Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected only if the option for the fourth Season is exercised by the Team.
CC. The Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
DD. The Base Salary for the second Season is never protected for Lottery picks; it remains conditional throughout.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i) provides that for a Lottery pick's Rookie Scale Contract, the first Season is fully protected outright, while the second Season Base Salary becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract. The third and fourth Seasons become fully protected only upon exercise of the fourth-year option.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly five (5) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA. She is eligible because she has at least four (4) Years of Service.
BB. She is eligible because she has more than four (4) but fewer than six (6) Years of Service, which is the threshold.
CC. She is ineligible because Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) requires at least six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing.
DD. She is eligible only if the Contract is an Extension of an existing Contract, not a new Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly provides that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly five (5) Years of Service has fewer than six (6) Years of Service and is therefore ineligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays four (4) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and three (3) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays two (2) Seasons under her Contract and one (1) Season under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA. Ten (10) Seasons total.
BB. Six (6) Seasons total, counting only the original Contract terms for both players.
CC. Seven (7) Seasons total: four (4) for Player A (Contract only, not Extension) plus three (3) for Player B (Contract plus Extension).
DD. Four (4) Seasons total, counting only Player A's original Contract Seasons since Player B's designation is precluded.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), only Seasons played under a Contract signed while a player was designated as a Core Player—but NOT Seasons under any Extension to that Contract—count toward the Team's Core Player designation limit. For Player A, only four (4) Contract Seasons count (not the three Extension Seasons). For Player B, two (2) Contract Seasons plus one (1) Extension Season count, totaling three (3). Grand total: 4 + 3 = 7 Seasons.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly five (5) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA. 105% of the player's fourth-year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB. The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CC. The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD. The Supermax Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' provision (D) specifies that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer must be the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This applies to any Veteran Free Agent with four (4) or more Years of Service qualifying under Sec. 5(b).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA. The Shortfall is triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% from the prior year; the Shortfall equals the excess above a 13% increase multiplied by total Teams.
BB. The Shortfall is triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% from the prior year but for the cap on increases; the Shortfall equals the additional amount by which the Salary Cap would have increased above that 10% threshold, multiplied by the total number of Teams in the WNBA for that Season.
CC. The Shortfall is triggered whenever Total Salaries are less than the Salary Cap multiplied by total Teams; the Shortfall is the difference.
DD. The Shortfall is triggered when the Salary Cap decreases by any amount; the Shortfall equals the full decrease multiplied by total Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) states that the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap for a Salary Cap Year would have increased by more than ten percent (10%) of the prior year's Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); the 13% cap applies only to the 2027 Salary Cap Year. For 2032 the applicable threshold is 10%, and the Shortfall equals the amount of the excess above that 10% increase multiplied by the total number of WNBA Teams that Season.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states both the out-of-network annual deductible per covered individual AND the out-of-network coverage percentage for most eligible charges after the deductible is met but before the out-of-pocket threshold is reached?
AA. $2,500 deductible; 80% coverage for most eligible charges.
BB. $1,800 deductible; 60% coverage for most eligible charges.
CC. $2,500 deductible; 60% coverage for most eligible charges.
DD. $6,250 deductible; 70% coverage for most eligible charges.
CBA Reference
Art. Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses ten (10) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team imposes a fine of 2.5% of Base Salary for each of the ten (10) games, totaling 25% of Base Salary. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the application of the aggregate cap?
AA. The full 25% fine is valid because the 20% aggregate cap only applies to playoff game fines.
BB. The aggregate fine is capped at 20% of Base Salary for Regular Season games, so the Team may collect only 20% of Base Salary in fines under this provision, not 25%.
CC. The fine is capped at 25% of Base Salary for Regular Season games, matching the playoff cap, so 25% is permissible.
DD. The Team may impose the full 25% because fines for more than eight (8) games are exempt from the 20% aggregate cap.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly provides that in no event may aggregate fines for missing Regular Season games exceed twenty percent (20%) of the player's Base Salary in any Season. Even though 2.5% × 10 games = 25%, the aggregate is capped at 20% for Regular Season missed games. The 25% cap applies only to playoff games.

Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2029 Salary Cap Year?
AA. 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
BB. 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
CC. 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
DD. 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) expressly lists the 2029 Salary Cap Year formula as 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year. The 12.5% Team Revenue percentage applies to 2030, and 13% applies to 2031 and 2032.
Q10Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, a non-Developmental Player whose Base Salary is exactly $500,000 IS entitled to a Team-provided one-bedroom apartment in the home city of her WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a)(iii) provides that for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is 'less than or equal to $500,000' during the corresponding Season are entitled to Team-provided housing. A player earning exactly $500,000 falls within this threshold and is therefore entitled to the benefit.
