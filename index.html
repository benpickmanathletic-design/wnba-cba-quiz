Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its SECOND Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The Rookie Scale Contract counts against the per-team limit of seven (7) because the non-Lottery First Round Pick exception only applies in the first Season.
BB) The Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the per-team limit of seven (7), because the exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract, provided it has not been extended.
CC) The Rookie Scale Contract counts against the per-team limit of seven (7) only if the Base Salary for the second Season has been renegotiated pursuant to Article VII, Section 5(c)(ii).
DD) The Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the per-team limit of seven (7) only if the player's Base Salary for the second Season is not yet fully protected.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), any Rookie Scale Contract that contains Base Salary protection (including renegotiated Base Salary) shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection; this exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and ceases only if the Contract is extended.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the THIRD and FOURTH Seasons of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected automatically at the start of the third Regular Season covered by the Contract.
BB) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected only if the option for the fourth Season is exercised by the Team.
CC) The Base Salary for the third Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested prior to the first day of the third Regular Season, while the fourth Season becomes protected only upon exercise of the option.
DD) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons is never protected for a Lottery First Round Pick unless separately negotiated in Exhibit 2.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i)(3) states that for a Lottery First Round Pick, the Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons shall become fully protected if the option for the fourth Season of such Contract is exercised by the Team; there is no independent protection trigger based solely on the start of the third Season.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly three (3) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible to include a trade bonus because three (3) Years of Service satisfies the six-Year threshold.
BB) She is not eligible to include a trade bonus because her Contract or Extension is entered into when she has fewer than six (6) Years of Service.
CC) She is eligible to include a trade bonus only if her new Contract is an Extension of an existing Contract.
DD) She is eligible to include a trade bonus only if the trade bonus does not exceed five percent (5%) of her remaining Base Salary.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly provides that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus; three (3) Years of Service falls below this threshold.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays five (5) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and two (2) Seasons under an Extension. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays one (1) Season under her Contract and three (3) Seasons under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Eleven (11) Seasons — all Seasons played by both players under both Contracts and Extensions.
BB) Six (6) Seasons — five (5) for Player A (Contract only) and one (1) for Player B (Contract only), because Extension Seasons are excluded.
CC) Eight (8) Seasons — five (5) for Player A (Contract only) plus two (2) for her Extension, but Player B contributes only one (1).
DD) Seven (7) Seasons — five (5) for Player A (Contract only, excluding Extension) and two (2) for Player B (one Contract Season plus one Extension Season).
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) specifies that only Seasons played while under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the maximum, explicitly excluding Seasons covered by any Extension; therefore Player A contributes five (5) Seasons and Player B contributes one (1) Season, totaling six (6).
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly nine (9) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's fourth year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary applicable to the Salary Cap Year of the Qualifying Offer.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Supermax Salary applicable to the Salary Cap Year of the Qualifying Offer.
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subsection (D), a Restricted Qualifying Offer for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract; this applies regardless of Years of Service for Veteran Free Agents qualifying under Sec. 5(b).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2029 would have increased by more than 13% from the 2028 Salary Cap but for the cap; the Shortfall equals the excess increase multiplied by the number of Teams in the 2029 Season.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2029 would have increased by more than 10% from the 2028 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the additional amount by which the Salary Cap would have increased (i.e., the amount in excess of a 10% increase) multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2029 Season.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries for 2029 are less than the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams; the Shortfall equals the difference.
DD) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2029 would have decreased by more than 10% from the 2028 Salary Cap; the Shortfall equals the decrease amount multiplied by the number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap for a given year (other than 2027, which has a 13% cap) would have increased by more than 10% but for the limit in Sec. 1(a)(ii); the 2029 Salary Cap Year uses the standard 10% threshold, and the Shortfall equals the excess above 10% multiplied by the total number of Teams that Season.
Q7Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2031 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) states that for each of the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years, the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals 30.5% of League Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year; for 2031, that means 30.5% of 2030 League Revenue plus 13% of 2030 Team Revenue.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses three (3) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her the maximum 2.5% of Base Salary per game for all three games (totaling 7.5% of Base Salary). The WNBA then separately seeks to impose an additional fine of 7% of Base Salary for those same three missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the permissibility of the WNBA's additional fine?
AA) The WNBA's additional fine of 7% is permissible because the combined total of 14.5% does not exceed the 20% aggregate cap.
BB) The WNBA's additional fine of 7% is impermissible because once the Team has imposed a fine under Sec. 7 for the same missed games, neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose an additional fine on the player for those same games.
CC) The WNBA's additional fine of 7% is permissible because the WNBA and the Team are separate disciplinary entities and each may independently fine a player.
DD) The WNBA's additional fine of 7% is impermissible only if it would cause aggregate fines to exceed 25% of Base Salary.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 states explicitly that if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine pursuant to Sec. 7 for missed Regular Season or playoff games, neither the Team nor the WNBA, as the case may be, shall be permitted to impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games; because the Team already fined her, the WNBA cannot add another fine for those same games.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000 are entitled to Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing in their WNBA Team's home city.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) limits the $500,000 Base Salary threshold for Team-provided housing to the 2029 and 2030 Seasons only; for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, the CBA makes no provision for Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing for any players under that threshold, as those Seasons are not listed in the housing schedule.
Q10Art. VIII Sec. (c)4-choice
Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, what is the adjustment mechanism for Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks?
AA) Second and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by the same percentage increase or decrease in the Salary Cap as First Round Picks.
BB) Second and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are increased by a flat 4% each Season, regardless of any change in the Salary Cap.
CC) Second and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are increased by the greater of 4% or the percentage increase in the Salary Cap from the prior Season.
DD) Second and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts remain fixed at $270,000 for all Seasons after 2026 and are not subject to any adjustment.
CBA Reference
Art. VIII, Sec. (c) states that for Second Round and Third Round Picks, the Rookie Scale Amounts for Contracts commencing after 2026 are adjusted by increasing the immediately prior Season's amounts by 4%, which is a fixed percentage independent of any Salary Cap movement that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
