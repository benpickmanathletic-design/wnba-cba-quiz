Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery' is currently in its THIRD Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection at this point in time?
AA) The exception no longer applies because the Lottery pick's Base Salary became fully protected only after the option for the fourth Season was exercised, ending the exception at that moment.
BB) The exception still applies because the Contract remains within its original four-Season Rookie Scale term and has not yet been extended.
CC) The exception ceased to apply at the start of the third Season because the CBA limits the exception to only the first two Seasons of a Lottery pick's Rookie Scale Contract.
DD) The exception never applied to Lottery picks because their Base Salary is fully protected from day one, making the exception unnecessary.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), the exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection applies to any Rookie Scale Contract 'during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract' and expressly does NOT apply 'with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.' Because this contract is in its third Season and has not been extended, the exception remains active.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the THIRD and FOURTH Seasons of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons is fully protected from the moment the Rookie Scale Contract is signed, with no conditions attached.
BB) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected only if the option for the fourth Season of the Contract is exercised by the Team.
CC) The Base Salary for the third Season is fully protected if waivers are not requested before the third Regular Season, and the fourth Season is protected upon option exercise.
DD) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons is never protected under a Lottery pick's Rookie Scale Contract; protection must be separately negotiated.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i)(3) states that for a Lottery pick's Rookie Scale Contract, the Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons 'shall become fully protected if the option for the fourth Season of such Contract is exercised by the Team.' There is no separate condition for the third Season distinct from option exercise.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly two (2) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible because the trade bonus provision only bars players with fewer than six (6) Years of Service from including a trade bonus in an Extension, not a new Contract.
BB) She is ineligible because Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) bars any Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service from containing a trade bonus.
CC) She is eligible because the six (6) Year of Service threshold only applies to players who are Unrestricted Free Agents at the time of signing.
DD) She is ineligible only if the Contract is a Rookie Scale Contract; a veteran Contract at two (2) Years of Service may include a trade bonus.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) provides that 'No Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing such Contract or Extension, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a [trade bonus].' With only two (2) Years of Service, she falls below the six (6) year threshold and is therefore ineligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays two (2) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and three (3) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays four (4) Seasons under her Contract (no extension). How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Nine (9) Seasons total: five (5) for Player A (2 + 3) and four (4) for Player B.
BB) Six (6) Seasons total: two (2) for Player A (only the non-extension Seasons count) and four (4) for Player B.
CC) Five (5) Seasons total: one (1) for Player A and four (4) for Player B, because only the first Season of each player's designated Contract counts.
DD) Four (4) Seasons total: only Player B's Seasons count because Player A's designation was superseded by Player B's designation.
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) specifies that only the Seasons played 'while such player was so designated (but not including Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract)' count toward the maximum Core Player designation limit. For Player A, only the 2 non-extension Seasons count; the 3 extension Seasons do not. Player B has 4 Seasons with no extension, all of which count. Total: 2 + 4 = 6.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly seven (7) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or 105% of the player's fourth year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB) The applicable Standard Maximum Salary for the relevant Salary Cap Year.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The applicable Minimum Player Salary for a player with 1-3 Years of Service, regardless of actual Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' subsection (D) states that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer must be 'the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.' This applies to all Veteran Free Agents qualifying under Sec. 5(b), regardless of the specific number of Years of Service above four (4).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2033 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2033 would have increased by more than 13% from the 2032 Salary Cap but for the cap on increases; the Shortfall equals the excess amount above 13% multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2033 would have increased by more than 10% from the 2032 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the additional amount above a 10% increase, multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2033 Season.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries for 2033 are less than the 2033 Salary Cap multiplied by the total number of Teams; the Shortfall equals the difference.
DD) Triggered when the Designated 20% Share of SBR for 2033 falls below the prior year's SBR Share; the Shortfall equals 20% of the difference multiplied by the number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap 'would have increased by more than ten percent (10%) of the Salary Cap for the Salary Cap Year immediately preceding such Salary Cap Year... but for the operation of Section 1(a)(ii).' The 13% threshold applies only to the 2027 Cap Year. For 2033, the 10% rule applies, and the Shortfall equals the excess above a 10% increase multiplied by total Teams.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the out-of-network coverage percentage for most eligible charges AFTER the deductible is met but BEFORE the out-of-pocket threshold is reached, AND the family out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) 70% for most eligible charges; the family out-of-pocket threshold is $20,000 per family.
BB) 60% for most eligible charges; the family out-of-pocket threshold is $20,000 per family.
CC) 80% for most eligible charges; the family out-of-pocket threshold is $9,000 per family.
DD) 60% for most eligible charges; the family out-of-pocket threshold is $9,000 per family.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) specifies that for non-Toronto Tempo players, out-of-network services cover 'sixty percent (60%) for most eligible charges... until the covered individual has paid $8,000 per person ($20,000 per family) in a calendar year, and one-hundred percent (100%) thereafter.' The 70% rate applies only to certain other eligible charges as specified in the plan, not 'most' eligible charges.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses four (4) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team imposes a 2.5%-per-game fine for all four games, totaling 10% of Base Salary. The WNBA then separately seeks to impose an additional fine (not a suspension) of 15% of Base Salary for those same four missed games. Which of the following is CORRECT?
AA) The WNBA may impose the additional 15% fine because the 20% aggregate cap only applies to fines imposed by the Team, not by the WNBA.
BB) The WNBA may not impose any additional fine for those same missed games because Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that if a fine is imposed pursuant to Sec. 7, neither the Team nor the WNBA shall be permitted to impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games.
CC) The WNBA may impose an additional fine of up to 10% of Base Salary for those same missed games, because the 20% aggregate cap permits a combined total up to 20%.
DD) The WNBA may impose the additional 15% fine because only one fining entity is bound by the 20% cap at a time, and the Team has already used its allotment.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 states with clarity that 'if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine pursuant to this Section 7 in respect of missed Regular Season or playoff games, neither the Team nor the WNBA, as the case may be, shall be permitted to impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games.' Once the Team imposes the Sec. 7 fine, neither entity may add another fine for those same games.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary exceeds $500,000 are entitled to Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing in the home city of their WNBA Team under the same income-threshold rule that applies for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) provides housing rules only for the 2026-2028 Seasons (all non-Developmental Players) and the 2029-2030 Seasons (those with Base Salary ≤ $500,000). The CBA provides NO express Team-provided housing entitlement for non-Developmental Players in the 2031 and 2032 Seasons under the $500,000 threshold or any other threshold—those Seasons are not covered by subsections (ii) or (iii). Therefore, the statement is False.

Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2028 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
