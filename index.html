Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick (i.e., a First Round Pick NOT selected in the Lottery) is currently in its FIRST Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception does NOT apply because only Lottery picks' Rookie Scale Contracts are exempt from the seven-contract limit.
BB) The exception applies and the non-Lottery First Round Pick's Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection, provided the Contract has not been extended.
CC) The exception applies only after waivers are not requested prior to the first day of the first Regular Season, at which point protection vests and the exemption begins.
DD) The exception does not apply during the first Season because the Base Salary protection for the first Season is only conditional until the start of the first Regular Season.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) provides that any Rookie Scale Contract containing Base Salary protection shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection. This exception applies to all Rookie Scale Contracts with any Base Salary protection (including conditional protection) during the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and does not apply during any extended term. A non-Lottery First Round Pick's Rookie Scale Contract in its first Season (not extended) falls squarely within this exception regardless of whether protection has vested.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick (a First Round Pick NOT selected in the Lottery), which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the first Season is fully protected from the date of signing.
BB) The Base Salary for the first Season contains no protection of any kind.
CC) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract.
DD) The Base Salary for the first Season becomes fully protected only if the fourth-year option is exercised by the Team.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(ii) provides that for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by any First Round Pick other than a Lottery pick, the Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract. This contrasts with Lottery picks (immediately fully protected for Season 1) and Second/Third Round Picks (no protection at all for Season 1).
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly four (4) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is eligible because she has at least four (4) Years of Service, which meets the six-year threshold.
BB) She is ineligible because she has fewer than six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing.
CC) She is eligible, but the trade bonus may not exceed five percent (5%) of the Base Salary remaining under the Contract.
DD) She is eligible only if the Contract is also an Extension of a prior Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly states that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. With exactly four (4) Years of Service, this player falls below the six-year threshold and is therefore ineligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays three (3) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and zero (0) Seasons under any Extension. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays one (1) Season under her Contract and three (3) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Four (4) Seasons total: three (3) for Player A and one (1) for Player B.
BB) Seven (7) Seasons total: three (3) for Player A and four (4) for Player B.
CC) Six (6) Seasons total: three (3) for Player A and three (3) for Player B (Extension Seasons excluded).
DD) Five (5) Seasons total: three (3) for Player A and two (2) for Player B.
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) provides that only the Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the maximum, explicitly excluding Seasons covered by any Extension. Player A contributes three (3) Seasons (all under the designated Contract, no Extension). Player B contributes only one (1) Season (the Season under the designated Contract; the three Extension Seasons are excluded). Total: four (4).
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly eleven (11) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's fourth-year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Supermax Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subsection (D), provides that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer must be at least the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This applies regardless of Years of Service beyond the four-year threshold.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than ten percent (10%) but for the cap on increases; the Shortfall equals the excess amount multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than thirteen percent (13%) from the 2026 Salary Cap but for Art. VII, Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the additional amount by which the Salary Cap would have increased (i.e., the amount in excess of a thirteen percent (13%) increase) multiplied by the total number of Teams participating in the WNBA in the Season covered by the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries for the Salary Cap Year are less than the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams; the Shortfall equals that difference.
DD) Triggered when the Salary Cap decreases by more than thirteen percent (13%) from the prior year; the Shortfall equals the decrease multiplied by the number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) provides that for the 2027 Salary Cap Year specifically, the Salary Cap may not increase by more than thirteen percent (13%) from the 2026 Salary Cap. Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) then provides that the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than that 13% threshold but for the cap, with the Shortfall equaling the excess amount multiplied by the number of participating Teams.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the in-network annual deductible per covered individual AND the in-network family deductible?
AA) $600 per covered individual and $1,200 per family.
BB) $2,500 per covered individual and $6,250 per family.
CC) $600 per covered individual and $1,800 per family.
DD) $1,000 per covered individual and $3,000 per family.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) states that for in-network services, the plan provides a $600 annual deductible per covered individual and $1,800 per family, with coverage at 80% after the deductible until the out-of-pocket threshold is reached.
Q8Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, the CBA explicitly provides Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing to non-Developmental Players in the home city of their WNBA Team, applying the same $500,000 Base Salary income threshold that governs the 2029 and 2030 Seasons.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) provides Team-funded housing for all non-Developmental Players in 2026-2028 (no salary threshold), and for non-Developmental Players with Base Salary ≤$500,000 in 2029 and 2030 only. The CBA contains no provision extending Team-provided housing for the 2031 or 2032 Seasons; those Seasons are simply not addressed by the housing subsections.
Q9Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses seven (7) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team imposes the maximum 2.5%-per-game fine for all seven games (totaling 17.5% of Base Salary). The WNBA then separately seeks to impose a suspension for those same seven missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the interaction of the 20% aggregate fine cap with the suspension salary reduction?
AA) The 20% aggregate cap applies to both the fines and the suspension salary reduction combined, so the suspension salary reduction may not exceed 2.5% of Base Salary.
BB) The 20% aggregate cap limits only the fines imposed under Sec. 7; the salary reduction resulting from the separately imposed suspension is not subject to the 20% cap and may be imposed in addition to the 17.5% fine.
CC) Once the Team has imposed 17.5% in fines, neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose any additional monetary penalty of any kind, including a suspension, for those same games.
DD) The suspension salary reduction is capped at 2.5% per game regardless of the 20% aggregate fine cap.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 states explicitly that the 20% (Regular Season) and 25% (playoffs) caps on aggregate fines do not apply to lost salary resulting from a separately imposed suspension. The CBA clarifies that nothing in Sec. 7 limits a Team's or the WNBA's right to impose a suspension for the same missed games, and those suspension-related salary reductions fall outside the fine caps.
Q10Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2032 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) states that for each of the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years, the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals 30.5% of League Revenue for the Salary Cap Year immediately preceding the applicable Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for that same immediately preceding Salary Cap Year. For 2032, the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year is 2031.
