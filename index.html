Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract that has been EXTENDED ceases to benefit from the exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts containing Base Salary protection. During which precise window does the exception apply, and what event definitively ends it?
AA) The exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract AND during the extended term; it ends only when the player becomes a Veteran Free Agent.
BB) The exception applies only during the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract itself; it ceases to apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.
CC) The exception applies during the first two Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract only; it ceases upon the Team's exercise of the Fourth Year Option.
DD) The exception applies during the three initial Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract; it ceases upon exercise of the Fourth Year Option regardless of whether an extension is later signed.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) expressly states the exception applies only with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract; it does NOT apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended. Once an extension is signed, the contract counts against the seven-contract limit.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)(i)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the SECOND Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the second Season is fully protected from the date the Contract is signed.
BB) The Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected if the Team exercises the Fourth Year Option.
CC) The Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
DD) The Base Salary for the second Season is never protected under a Lottery Pick Rookie Scale Contract; only Seasons three and four receive protection upon option exercise.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i) states that for a Lottery Pick Rookie Scale Contract, the second Season's Base Salary becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by the Contract. This is distinct from the first Season, which is immediately fully protected.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player who has exactly five (5) Years of Service at the time of signing a new Contract wishes to include a trade bonus. Which answer CORRECTLY states her eligibility?
AA) She is eligible because the threshold is five (5) or more Years of Service.
BB) She is ineligible because the CBA requires at least six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing; five (5) Years of Service does not meet the threshold.
CC) She is eligible only if the Contract is simultaneously an Extension of an existing Player Contract.
DD) She is ineligible because trade bonuses are reserved exclusively for players with eight (8) or more Years of Service under the 2026 CBA.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) explicitly states that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly five (5) Years of Service does not meet the minimum threshold of six (6) Years.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays four (4) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and three (3) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays one (1) Season under her Contract (no extension). How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Eight (8) Seasons — all four of Player A's Contract Seasons, all three of her Extension Seasons, and Player B's one Season.
BB) Five (5) Seasons — four Seasons from Player A's Contract plus one Season from Player B's Contract; Extension Seasons are excluded.
CC) Seven (7) Seasons — all four of Player A's Contract Seasons plus all three of her Extension Seasons; Player B's Season is excluded because only one Core Player can be active at a time.
DD) Three (3) Seasons — Player A's Extension Seasons and Player B's one Season only; original Contract Seasons do not count.
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) states that only Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated (but NOT Seasons covered by any Extension) count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit. Therefore, Player A contributes four Seasons (Contract only, not the three Extension Seasons) and Player B contributes one Season, for a total of five (5).
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly five (5) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary from the last Season of her prior Contract.
BB) 110% of the applicable Minimum Player Salary for the upcoming Season.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Standard Maximum Salary for the upcoming Season.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' subsection (D) provides that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer floor is the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary from the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This applies to any Veteran Free Agent with four or more Years of Service qualifying under Sec. 5(b).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(c)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(c), the Minimum Team Salary for a Salary Cap Year equals what percentage of the Salary Cap, and which of the following is the ONE recognized exception to the WNBA's obligation to cause a Team to make up any Minimum Team Salary shortfall?
AA) 90% of the Salary Cap; a Team in its first expansion Season is exempt.
BB) 85% of the Salary Cap; a Team that ceases operations prior to the conclusion of a WNBA Season is not required to meet the Minimum Team Salary obligation.
CC) 85% of the Salary Cap; a Team whose roster falls below twelve players due to injuries is exempt for that portion of the Season.
DD) 80% of the Salary Cap; a Team that has been granted a Hardship Exception during the Season is not required to meet the Minimum Team Salary obligation.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(c) sets the Minimum Team Salary at eighty-five percent (85%) of the Salary Cap. The sole recognized exception under Sec. 1(c)(ii) is that any Team that ceases operations prior to the conclusion of a WNBA Season shall not be required to meet the Minimum Team Salary obligation.

AA) $2,500 deductible; 70% coverage for most eligible charges.

Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) specifies that for out-of-network services, the annual deductible is $2,500 per covered individual and coverage is sixty percent (60%) for most eligible charges (with 70% for certain other eligible charges as set forth in the plan) until the out-of-pocket threshold is reached.

Q9Art. VIII Sec. (c)T/F
True or False: Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by the same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. VIII, Sec. (c) draws a clear distinction: First Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by the percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap, while Second Round Pick and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are instead increased by a fixed 4% per Season, regardless of Salary Cap movement.
Q10Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, the CBA expressly provides that non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000 are entitled to Team-provided one-bedroom apartments in the home city of their WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) provides Team-provided apartments for ALL non-Developmental Players in the 2026, 2027, and 2028 Seasons, and for non-Developmental Players with Base Salary at or below $500,000 only in the 2029 and 2030 Seasons. There is no corresponding express provision for the 2031 or 2032 Seasons, meaning no non-Developmental Players are entitled to Team-provided home-city housing in those years under this section.
