Q1Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player with exactly five (5) Years of Service at the time of signing a new Contract wishes to include a trade bonus in that Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states whether she is eligible and why?
AA) She is INELIGIBLE; trade bonuses require at least six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing the Contract or Extension.
BB) She is ELIGIBLE; the six (6) Years of Service threshold applies only to Extensions, not new Contracts.
CC) She is ELIGIBLE; the five (5) Years of Service threshold is sufficient under the 2026 CBA.
DD) She is INELIGIBLE; trade bonuses are only available to players with seven (7) or more Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly provides that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly five (5) Years of Service falls below this threshold and is therefore ineligible.
Q2Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection applies during which precise window AND under what specific condition does it definitively cease to apply?
AA) It applies during all four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract and ceases upon the player's selection in any subsequent WNBA Draft.
BB) It applies only during the first two Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract and ceases when the Team exercises the fourth-year option.
CC) It applies during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract and definitively ceases upon (and does not apply during) the extended term of any Extension of that Rookie Scale Contract.
DD) It applies during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract and during the extended term of any Extension, but ceases once the player reaches four (4) Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) states that the Rookie Scale Contract exception shall only apply with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract and shall not apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended. The exception ends entirely once an Extension is triggered.
Q3Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the THIRD and FOURTH Seasons of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected automatically on the first day of the third Regular Season covered by such Contract.
BB) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected only if the option for the fourth Season is exercised by the Team.
CC) The Base Salary for the third Season is fully protected unconditionally, while the fourth Season becomes protected only upon exercise of the option.
DD) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested prior to the first day of the third Regular Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i), for a Lottery Pick's Rookie Scale Contract, the Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected only if the option for the fourth Season of such Contract is exercised by the Team. No prior waiver deadline triggers protection for these two Seasons.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), the Seasons counted toward a Team's maximum Core Player designation limit are determined by which rule?
AA) All Seasons played by the Core Player under any Contract with the Team, including Seasons covered by any Extension, count toward the Team's limit.
BB) Only the Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count; Seasons covered by any Extension to that Contract do NOT count.
CC) Only the first three Seasons of the Contract signed while designated count, regardless of Extensions.
DD) The Seasons count only if the player was continuously on the active roster and not suspended during each such Season.
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) expressly states that any Seasons covered by a Contract signed while the player was so designated—but NOT including Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract—shall be included in the maximum number of players who may be designated as a Core Player by such Team.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly ten (10) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or 105% of the player's fourth-year Base Salary.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) 110% of the player's Base Salary in the last Season of her prior Contract.
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subsection (D) provides that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), a Restricted Qualifying Offer must be the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This rule applies regardless of Years of Service once the player qualifies under Sec. 5(b).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(ii), the 'Guarantee Shortfall' for a Salary Cap Year is triggered and computed under which of the following conditions?
AA) When the Salary Cap for a Salary Cap Year would have increased by more than ten percent (10%) but for the cap on annual increases; the shortfall equals the excess increase multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) When, as of the conclusion of a Season, Total Salaries for such Salary Cap Year are less than the Salary Cap for such Salary Cap Year multiplied by the total number of Teams; the shortfall equals the amount of that deficit.
CC) When a Team's Team Salary is less than eighty-five percent (85%) of the Salary Cap; the shortfall equals the difference between 85% of the Salary Cap and actual Team Salary.
DD) When Total Salaries exceed the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams; the shortfall equals the excess amount returned to Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(ii) provides that the Guarantee Shortfall arises when, as of the conclusion of a Season covered by a Salary Cap Year, Total Salaries for such year are less than the Salary Cap multiplied by the total number of Teams participating in the WNBA; the shortfall equals that deficit amount.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the in-network out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100% AND the out-of-network out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) In-network: $3,000 per person; out-of-network: $8,000 per person.
BB) In-network: $9,000 per family only; out-of-network: $20,000 per family only.
CC) In-network: $3,000 per person ($9,000 per family); out-of-network: $8,000 per person ($20,000 per family).
DD) In-network: $1,800 per family; out-of-network: $6,250 per family.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) states that for in-network services, coverage is at 80% until the covered individual has paid $3,000 per person ($9,000 per family) in a calendar year, then 100% thereafter; for out-of-network services, coverage reaches 100% after the covered individual pays $8,000 per person ($20,000 per family) in a calendar year.

Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2030 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.

Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, a non-Developmental Player whose Base Salary is exactly $500,001 (one dollar above $500,000) is entitled to a Team-provided one-bedroom apartment in the home city of her WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a)(iii) provides that for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, only non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000 are entitled to Team-provided housing. A player earning $500,001 exceeds the $500,000 threshold and is therefore not entitled to Team-provided housing under that provision.
Q10Art. XIV Sec. 7T/F
True or False: Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, the 20% aggregate Regular Season fine cap expressly DOES NOT limit any additional salary reduction resulting from a suspension imposed for the same missed Regular Season games, even if a 2.5%-per-game fine has already been imposed for those games.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that nothing in that Section shall be construed to limit a Team's right to impose a suspension for the same (or other) missed games, and that the 20% and 25% caps 'shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension.' The caps govern only the fines themselves, not any concurrent suspension-based salary reduction.
