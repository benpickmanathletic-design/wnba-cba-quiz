Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its THIRD Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception still applies, because the Contract is in its third Season and has not been extended, so it remains within the four-Season window.
BB) The exception no longer applies, because the Base Salary for the third Season becomes protected only upon exercise of the fourth-year option, meaning no Base Salary protection has yet attached for Season 3.
CC) The exception does not apply at all to non-Lottery First Round Picks; only Lottery Picks qualify for the exception.
DD) The exception applies only if the Team has already exercised the fourth-year option prior to the third Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), any Rookie Scale Contract that contains Base Salary protection shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) during the four Seasons of such Contract (and not during any extended term). A non-Lottery First Round Pick's third-Season Base Salary becomes fully protected only upon exercise of the option, but the exception to the seven-Contract limit applies to the Contract itself during any of its four Seasons regardless of whether protection has yet vested, as long as the Contract contains Base Salary protection of any kind. The key boundary is that the exception ceases upon the extended term, not before.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a non-Lottery First Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the THIRD and FOURTH Seasons of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the third Regular Season covered by such Contract.
BB) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected if the option for the fourth Season is exercised by the Team.
CC) The Base Salary for the third Season is immediately fully protected upon signing, while the fourth Season Base Salary becomes protected only upon exercise of the option.
DD) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(ii), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by any First Round Pick other than a Lottery Pick, the Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons shall become fully protected if the option for the fourth Season of such Contract is exercised by the Team. This rule is identical to that which applies to Lottery Picks for Seasons three and four.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly six (6) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is ineligible, because the trade bonus provision requires more than six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing.
BB) She is eligible, because the provision requires at least six (6) Years of Service and she has exactly six (6) at the time of signing.
CC) She is ineligible, because trade bonuses may only be included in Extensions, not in new Contracts.
DD) She is eligible, but only if the Contract is for more than one Season.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) states that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly six (6) Years of Service does not have fewer than six, so she satisfies the threshold and is eligible to include a trade bonus.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays one (1) Season under the Contract signed while designated and five (5) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player, and she plays three (3) Seasons under her Contract and two (2) Seasons under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 11 Seasons total (6 for Player A + 5 for Player B).
BB) 4 Seasons total (1 for Player A + 3 for Player B), because Extension Seasons are excluded.
CC) 6 Seasons total (1 for Player A + 5 for Player B), because only Contract Seasons (not Extension Seasons) are counted.
DD) 9 Seasons total (6 for Player A + 3 for Player B), counting all Seasons for Player A but only Contract Seasons for Player B.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), only the Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the Team's maximum designation limit; Seasons covered by any Extension to that Contract are expressly excluded. Therefore, only Player A's 1 Season (under her original Contract) and Player B's 3 Seasons (under her original Contract) count, for a total of 4 Seasons.

Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly six (6) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) The Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal 105% of the player's fourth-year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB) The Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
CC) The Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal the Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
DD) The Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal the Core Qualifying Offer amount (i.e., the Maximum Player Salary).
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subpart (D), for a player subject to a right of first refusal in accordance with Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This applies to all Veteran Free Agents qualifying under Sec. 5(b), regardless of their specific Years of Service.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2028 would have increased by more than 13% from the 2027 Salary Cap but for the cap on increases; Shortfall = excess above 13% multiplied by total number of Teams in 2028.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2028 would have increased by more than 10% from the 2027 Salary Cap but for the cap on increases; Shortfall = excess above 10% multiplied by total number of Teams in 2028.
CC) Triggered when Total Salaries for 2028 are less than the Salary Cap for 2028 multiplied by the total number of Teams; Shortfall = that deficit amount.
DD) Triggered when the Salary Cap for 2028 would have decreased by more than 10% from the 2027 Salary Cap; Shortfall = excess decrease above 10% multiplied by total number of Teams in 2028.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for Salary Cap Years following the 2027 Salary Cap Year (where the threshold is 13%), the 10% cap applies. For the 2028 Salary Cap Year, a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises only if the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% over the 2027 Salary Cap but for Art. VII, Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the excess amount above that 10% threshold multiplied by the total number of Teams participating in the WNBA in the 2028 Season.

Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the in-network coverage percentage AFTER the deductible is met but BEFORE the individual out-of-pocket threshold is reached, AND the per-person in-network out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) 70% in-network coverage after deductible; $8,000 per-person threshold for 100% coverage.

CC) 80% in-network coverage after deductible; $9,000 per-family threshold is the only stated threshold.

Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses eight (8) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team imposes the maximum 2.5%-per-game fine for all eight games, totaling 20% of Base Salary. The WNBA then separately seeks to impose an additional fine (not a suspension) of 5% of Base Salary for the same eight missed games. Which of the following is CORRECT?
AA) The WNBA may impose the additional 5% fine because the 20% aggregate cap applies only to fines imposed by the Team, not by the WNBA.
BB) The WNBA may not impose any additional fine for those same missed games, because Art. XIV, Sec. 7 provides that once the Team (or WNBA) imposes a fine for missed Regular Season games, neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose an additional fine for the same missed games.
CC) The WNBA may impose up to an additional 5% fine because the 20% cap applies only per-entity, not in aggregate across both the WNBA and the Team.
DD) The WNBA may impose the additional fine, but only up to 2.5% per game, capped at 20% total across all fines for Regular Season games in that Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, for clarity subpart (i), if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine pursuant to Sec. 7 in respect of missed Regular Season games, neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games. The 20% aggregate cap has already been reached, and the prohibition on double-fining for the same missed games independently bars any additional fine by the WNBA.
Q9Art. VIII Sec. (c)T/F
True or False: Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by the same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for First Round Picks, Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by the percentage increase or decrease in the Salary Cap from one year to the next. However, for Second and Third Round Picks (and 'All Others'), the Rookie Scale Amounts are instead increased by a flat 4% per Season, not by the Salary Cap percentage change. These are two distinct adjustment mechanisms.
Q10Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2030 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), the Designated 20% Share of SBR for the 2030 Salary Cap Year is defined as 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year. This is distinct from the 2029 formula (which uses 12.25% of Team Revenue) and the 2031/2032 formula (which uses 13% of Team Revenue).
