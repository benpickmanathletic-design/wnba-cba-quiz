Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery' is currently in its THIRD Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The Rookie Scale Contract counts against the per-team limit of seven (7) protected contracts because the Lottery exception expires after the second Season.
BB) The Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the per-team limit of seven (7) protected contracts, because the exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and the Contract has not yet been extended.
CC) The Rookie Scale Contract does NOT count against the per-team limit only if the Base Salary was NOT renegotiated pursuant to Art. VII, Sec. 5(c)(ii); if renegotiated, it counts against the limit.
DD) The Rookie Scale Contract counts against the per-team limit because the exception only applies during the first two Seasons of a Lottery pick's Rookie Scale Contract.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), any Rookie Scale Contract containing Base Salary protection (including one with a renegotiated Base Salary) shall NOT count against the per-team limit of seven (7) protected contracts. This exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and explicitly does not apply to or during the extended term of any extension. Since the contract is in its third Season and has not been extended, the exception still applies.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the THIRD and FOURTH Seasons of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected only if the player was named to the All-WNBA team in either of the first two Seasons.
BB) The Base Salary for the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected automatically at the start of each respective Season if no waiver request has been made.
CC) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected if the option for the fourth Season of the Contract is exercised by the Team.
DD) Such a Rookie Scale Contract may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind for the third and fourth Seasons; only the second Season may be protected.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(iii), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, the Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected only if the Team exercises the option for the fourth Season. The first Season expressly may not contain any Base Salary protection.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly two (2) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) The player is eligible to include a trade bonus because she has completed at least one Year of Service.
BB) The player is not eligible to include a trade bonus because a player must have at least six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing to include a trade bonus.
CC) The player is eligible to include a trade bonus only if the Contract is for more than two Seasons.
DD) The player is eligible to include a trade bonus, but it may not exceed five percent (5%) of the Base Salary remaining under the Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly states that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. With only two Years of Service, this player is ineligible to include a trade bonus.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays two (2) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and three (3) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays four (4) Seasons under her Contract and one (1) Season under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Ten (10) Seasons total: five for Player A (2+3) and five for Player B (4+1).
BB) Six (6) Seasons total: two for Player A (only non-extension Seasons count) and four for Player B (only non-extension Seasons count).
CC) Seven (7) Seasons total: two for Player A (only non-extension Seasons count) and five for Player B (4+1, both count).
DD) Five (5) Seasons total: two for Player A (only non-extension Seasons count) and three for Player B (only non-extension Seasons count).
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) specifies that only Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the maximum—Seasons covered by any Extension do NOT count. Player A contributes 2 Seasons (not the 3 extension Seasons) and Player B contributes 4 Seasons (not the 1 extension Season), for a total of 6 Seasons.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly seven (7) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary from the last Season of her prior Contract.
BB) The applicable Standard Maximum Salary.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The applicable Supermax Salary.
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' subsection (D), a Restricted Qualifying Offer for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This rule applies regardless of Years of Service for Veteran Free Agents qualifying under that subsection.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) It is triggered when the Salary Cap for the 2032 Year would have increased by more than 13% over the 2031 Salary Cap but for the cap on increases; the Shortfall equals the excess amount above a 13% increase multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) It is triggered when the Salary Cap for the 2032 Year would have increased by more than 10% over the 2031 Salary Cap but for Art. VII, Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the additional amount above a 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2032 Season.
CC) It is triggered when Total Salaries league-wide fall below the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams; the Shortfall equals that deficit.
DD) It is triggered when any individual Team's Team Salary falls below 85% of the Salary Cap; the Shortfall equals the sum of all individual Team shortfalls.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap for a given Salary Cap Year would have increased by more than 10% (or 13% for 2027) over the prior year but for the cap on increases in Sec. 1(a)(ii). For the 2032 Salary Cap Year, the applicable threshold is 10% over the 2031 cap. The Shortfall equals the excess amount above that 10% increase, multiplied by the total number of Teams participating in the 2032 Season.

Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses four (4) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her 2.5% of Base Salary for each game (totaling 10% of Base Salary). The WNBA then separately seeks to impose a fine of 12% of Base Salary for those same four missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the permissibility of the WNBA's additional fine?
AA) The WNBA's additional fine of 12% is fully permissible because the 20% aggregate cap applies only to the Team's fines, not WNBA fines.
BB) The WNBA cannot impose any fine because the Team already fined the player; only one entity (Team or WNBA) may impose fines for missed Regular Season games.
CC) The WNBA may impose an additional fine, but the total combined fines from Team and WNBA cannot exceed 20% of Base Salary; therefore the WNBA's fine would be capped at 10% (bringing the total to 20%).
DD) The WNBA's additional fine is fully permissible and not subject to the 20% aggregate cap because the WNBA and Team fines are calculated separately.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 states that the Team or, if the Team declines, the WNBA may fine a player for missed Regular Season games, with the aggregate fines by either the WNBA or the Team not to exceed 20% of Base Salary in any Season. For clarity, if a fine is imposed under Sec. 7 for missed games, neither the Team nor the WNBA shall impose an additional fine for the same missed games. The Team already imposed 10%, so any additional WNBA fine is capped at 10% to stay within the 20% aggregate ceiling.
Q9Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2028 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2028 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) explicitly defines the Designated 20% Share of SBR for the 2028 Salary Cap Year as 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2027 Salary Cap Year. The 12.25% Team Revenue rate does not apply until the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
Q10Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2026, 2027, and 2028 Seasons, only non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000 are entitled to Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing in the home city of their WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a)(ii) provides that for each of the 2026, 2027, and 2028 Seasons, ALL non-Developmental Players (with no income threshold) will be provided with a one-bedroom apartment in the home city of their WNBA Team. The $500,000 Base Salary income threshold only begins to apply starting with the 2029 and 2030 Seasons under Sec. 1(a)(iii).
