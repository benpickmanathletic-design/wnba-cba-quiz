Q1Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player who has exactly three (3) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract wishes to include a trade bonus in that Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility?
AA) She is eligible because the six-Year-of-Service minimum applies only to Extensions, not new Contracts.
BB) She is ineligible because no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus.
CC) She is eligible provided the trade bonus does not exceed ten percent (10%) of remaining Base Salary.
DD) She is ineligible only if her Contract also contains an Exhibit 7 (simultaneous trade agreement).
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly states that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. With only three (3) Years of Service, she falls below that threshold and is therefore categorically ineligible.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the first Season is fully protected upon signing.
BB) The Base Salary for the first Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by such Contract.
CC) The Base Salary for the first Season becomes fully protected only if the Team exercises the option for the fourth Season.
DD) Such a Rookie Scale Contract may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind for the first Season.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(iii) explicitly states that a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind for the first Season. Protection only attaches for the second Season (waiver condition) and third/fourth Seasons (option exercise condition).
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick contains conditional Base Salary protection. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes when the exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection applies AND when it definitively ends?
AA) The exception applies for the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and definitively ends when the player is waived.
BB) The exception applies only during the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and definitively ends upon the commencement of the extended term if the Rookie Scale Contract is extended.
CC) The exception applies only during the first two Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and ends when the option for the fourth Season is exercised.
DD) The exception applies for all Seasons including any extended term, and ends only when the Contract expires.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) provides that the Rookie Scale Contract exception applies only with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract and expressly does NOT apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended. The exception therefore ends once the extended term commences.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays five (5) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and zero (0) Seasons under any Extension. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays two (2) Seasons under her Contract and two (2) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 7 Seasons (5 + 2)
BB) 9 Seasons (5 + 2 + 2)
CC) 4 Seasons (2 + 2)
DD) 5 Seasons (5 only, Extensions excluded)
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) states that only Seasons played under the Contract signed while designated (but NOT including Seasons covered by any Extension) count toward the maximum. For Player A, all 5 Seasons under her designated Contract count; for Player B, only the 2 Seasons under her Contract count (not the 2 Extension Seasons). Total = 5 + 2 = 7.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly nine (9) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
BB) The Supermax Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) 105% of the player's fourth year Base Salary under her most recent Rookie Scale Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' subsection (D) states that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), a Restricted Qualifying Offer must be the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. Years of Service beyond four do not change this formula for a Restricted Qualifying Offer under Sec. 5(b).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered and computed under which of the following conditions?
AA) The Salary Cap for the 2030 Salary Cap Year would have increased by more than 13% from the 2029 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the excess over 13% multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) The Salary Cap for the 2030 Salary Cap Year would have increased by more than 10% from the 2029 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the excess over 10% multiplied by the number of Teams.
CC) Total Salaries for the 2030 Salary Cap Year are less than the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams.
DD) Any Team's Team Salary falls below 85% of the Salary Cap for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% (or 13% for the 2027 Salary Cap Year only) but for the cap on increases under Sec. 1(a)(ii). The 2030 Salary Cap Year is subject to the 10% limit (not the 13% 2027-specific rule), and the Shortfall equals the excess amount multiplied by the total number of Teams.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)(i)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the out-of-network annual deductible per covered individual AND the out-of-network out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) $1,800 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after $9,000 out-of-pocket per person.
BB) $2,500 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after $8,000 out-of-pocket per person.
CC) $600 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after $3,000 out-of-pocket per person.
DD) $2,500 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after $6,250 out-of-pocket per person.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) specifies that for non-Toronto Tempo players, out-of-network services carry a $2,500 annual deductible per covered individual and coverage reaches 100% once the covered individual has paid $8,000 per person ($20,000 per family) in a calendar year.
Q8Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2031 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2030 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2031 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) specifies that for each of the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years, the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals 30.5% of League Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year. For 2031, the immediately preceding year is 2030.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, the CBA provides Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing to non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000, using the same income threshold that applies for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) specifies housing entitlements for the 2026-2028 Seasons (all non-Developmental Players) and separately for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons (non-Developmental Players with Base Salary ≤ $500,000). The CBA contains no housing provision for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons extending that entitlement, meaning no equivalent provision is stated for those years.
Q10Art. XIV Sec. 7T/F
True or False: Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine pursuant to the 2.5%-per-game provision for missed Regular Season games, the 20% aggregate Regular Season fine cap does NOT limit any salary reduction resulting from a suspension separately imposed on the player for those same missed Regular Season games.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that 'nothing in this Section 7 shall be construed to limit a Team's right, in circumstances where the WNBA or a Team has imposed 2½% or 5% fines in respect of missed games, to impose a suspension or other discipline on a player for the same (or other) missed games in accordance with this Agreement (and the 20% and 25% caps noted above shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension).' The suspension salary reduction is therefore not capped by the 20% fine cap.
