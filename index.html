Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery' is currently in its FOURTH Season (not extended). Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection at this point in time?
AA. The exception still applies because the Contract has not yet been extended and the fourth Season is a valid Season of the Rookie Scale Contract.
BB. The exception has expired because it only applies through the third Season of a Rookie Scale Contract, not the fourth.
CC. The exception no longer applies because the fourth Season is an Option Year, which constitutes an extension of the Contract for purposes of this rule.
DD. The exception applies only if the Team has exercised the Fourth Year Option before the start of the fourth Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the seven (7) protected-contract limit applies 'with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract' and ends only upon the extended term of any extension. The fourth Season (Option Year) is explicitly one of the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract itself, so the exception remains in effect through the entire fourth Season so long as the Contract has not been extended.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA. The Base Salary for the first Season is conditionally protected and becomes fully protected only if waivers are not requested prior to the first day of the first Regular Season.
BB. The Base Salary for the first Season is fully protected upon execution of the Contract with no conditions.
CC. The Base Salary for the first Season is not protected in any form until the Team exercises the Fourth Year Option.
DD. The Base Salary for the first Season becomes fully protected only if the player makes the opening-day roster.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' the Base Salary for the first Season 'shall be fully protected' — there is no conditional language, making it immediately and unconditionally fully protected upon signing.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly seven (7) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA. She is ineligible because the trade bonus provision requires at least eight (8) Years of Service.
BB. She is eligible because she has at least six (6) Years of Service, which is the minimum threshold for trade bonus eligibility.
CC. She is eligible only if the Contract is also an Extension of a prior Contract.
DD. She is ineligible because players must have completed their Rookie Scale Contract and have at least eight (8) Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) states that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly seven (7) Years of Service meets the minimum threshold of six (6) Years of Service and is therefore eligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays two (2) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and four (4) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays one (1) Season under her Contract and two (2) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA. Nine (9) Seasons total: six from Player A and three from Player B.
BB. Three (3) Seasons total: two from Player A (non-extended only) and one from Player B (non-extended only).
CC. Five (5) Seasons total: two from Player A (non-extended Seasons) and three from Player B (one non-extended plus two extended).
DD. Six (6) Seasons total: two from Player A and four from Player B's extended term.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), only the Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the maximum — Seasons covered by any Extension expressly do not count. Player A contributes two (2) Seasons (not the four extension Seasons) and Player B contributes one (1) Season (not the two extension Seasons), for a total of three (3) Seasons.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly eight (8) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA. 105% of her fourth year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB. The applicable Standard Maximum Salary for the upcoming Season.
CC. The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of her prior Contract.
DD. The applicable Supermax Salary for the upcoming Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subsection (D), a Restricted Qualifying Offer for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) must be the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. This rule applies regardless of the player's specific number of Years of Service beyond four (4).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2026 Salary Cap Year, which of the following CORRECTLY describes the conditions under which a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' could arise?
AA. A Salary Cap Limit Shortfall for the 2026 Salary Cap Year can arise if the computed Salary Cap would have exceeded the $7,000,000 figure by more than 13%.
BB. A Salary Cap Limit Shortfall cannot arise for the 2026 Salary Cap Year because the Salary Cap for that year is fixed at $7,000,000 by the CBA and is not derived from SBR calculations subject to a cap-limit ceiling.
CC. A Salary Cap Limit Shortfall for 2026 arises whenever Total Salaries league-wide fall below the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams.
DD. A Salary Cap Limit Shortfall for 2026 arises if the Designated 20% Share of SBR exceeds $7,000,000 per team.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(a)(i) fixes the 2026 Salary Cap at $7,000,000 outright; the SBR-based formula and the 13%/10% cap increase limits apply beginning with the 2027 Salary Cap Year. Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) only generates a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall when the cap would have increased beyond the applicable percentage ceiling 'but for the operation of Section 1(a)(ii),' and Section 1(a)(ii) has no operative effect on the fixed 2026 cap.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the in-network out-of-pocket threshold per person AND the family out-of-pocket threshold at which in-network coverage reaches 100%?
AA. $3,000 per person and $6,000 per family.
BB. $3,000 per person and $9,000 per family.
CC. $8,000 per person and $20,000 per family.
DD. $2,500 per person and $6,250 per family.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) specifies that for in-network services, coverage is at 80% until the covered individual has paid $3,000 per person ($9,000 per family) in a calendar year, after which coverage becomes 100%. The $8,000/$20,000 figures apply to out-of-network services.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses three (3) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her 2.5% of Base Salary for each game (totaling 7.5%). The WNBA then separately seeks to impose a suspension for those same three missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the interaction between the fine cap and the suspension salary reduction?
AA. The suspension salary reduction is barred because the Team already imposed a fine for the same missed games, triggering the 'no double penalty' rule.
BB. The 20% aggregate cap on Regular Season fines does NOT limit any salary reduction resulting from the separately imposed suspension, and both the fine and the suspension salary reduction may apply to the same missed games.
CC. The suspension salary reduction is limited to the difference between 20% and the 7.5% already fined, capping the total salary loss at 20%.
DD. Neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose a suspension once a fine has been collected for the same missed games.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that 'nothing in this Section 7 shall be construed to limit a Team's right, in circumstances where the WNBA or a Team has imposed 2½% or 5% fines in respect of missed games, to impose a suspension or other discipline on a player for the same (or other) missed games' and that 'the 20% and 25% caps noted above shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension.' The fine cap and the suspension salary reduction are therefore independent.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2033 Season (the final Season of the CBA), non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000 are explicitly guaranteed Team-provided housing in the home city of their WNBA Team under the same income-threshold rule that applies for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) specifies Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing for all non-Developmental Players for the 2026, 2027, and 2028 Seasons, and then provides income-based housing (≤$500,000) only for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons. The CBA makes no provision extending Team-provided housing to any Season beyond 2030, including the 2031, 2032, or 2033 Seasons.
Q10Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2033 Salary Cap Year?
AA. 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
BB. 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
CC. 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
DD. 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2033 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2033 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) provides that for each of the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years, the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals 30.5% of League Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for the immediately preceding Salary Cap Year. The CBA term runs through the 2033 Salary Cap Year, and the 2031-2032 formula (30.5%/13%) governs the 2033 Salary Cap Year as the most recent specified rule, using 2032 figures as the immediately preceding year.
