Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected IN the Lottery contains fully protected Base Salary. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the scope AND the end of the exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and also during any extended term if the Contract is later extended.
BB) The exception applies only during the initial four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and ends definitively upon the commencement of any extended term of that Contract.
CC) The exception applies only during the first two Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and ends when the Team exercises or waives the fourth-year option.
DD) The exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract and continues for up to one additional Season if the player is re-signed immediately after the Rookie Scale Contract expires.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) expressly states that the exception from the seven-contract limit shall only apply 'with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract' and 'shall not apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.' There is no carryover into any extended term.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the THIRD and FOURTH Seasons of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected when waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the third Regular Season covered by the Contract.
BB) Such a Rookie Scale Contract may not contain any Base Salary protection of any kind for the third or fourth Seasons.
CC) The Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons becomes fully protected if the option for the fourth Season of such Contract is exercised by the Team.
DD) The Base Salary for the third Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested prior to the third Regular Season, and for the fourth Season upon the Team's exercise of the option.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(iii) provides that for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, 'the Base Salary for each of the third and fourth Seasons of such Contract shall become fully protected if the option for the fourth Season of such Contract is exercised by the Team.' Both the third and fourth Seasons are triggered by the same event: the Team's exercise of the fourth-year option.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), which of the following players is ELIGIBLE to have a trade bonus included in her Contract or Extension under the 2026 CBA?
AA) A player with exactly two (2) Years of Service at the time she signs the Contract.
BB) A player with exactly four (4) Years of Service at the time she signs the Contract.
CC) A player with exactly five (5) Years of Service at the time she signs the Contract.
DD) A player with exactly six (6) Years of Service at the time she signs the Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) expressly states that 'No Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing such Contract or Extension, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain' a trade bonus. A player with exactly six (6) Years of Service meets the minimum threshold and is therefore eligible.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays zero (0) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and six (6) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays three (3) Seasons under her Contract (no extension). How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) Nine (9) Seasons total.
BB) Six (6) Seasons total, because only the original Contract term (not any Extension) counts for Player A.
CC) Three (3) Seasons total, because only Player B's Seasons under a non-extended Contract count.
DD) Zero (0) Seasons total for Player A (since she played zero Seasons under the designated Contract) plus three (3) for Player B, equaling three (3) Seasons total.
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) states that only 'Seasons played pursuant to a Contract signed while such player was so designated (but not including Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract)' count toward the limit. Player A played zero Seasons under the designated Contract itself (all six were under an Extension), so zero count. Player B played three Seasons under her non-extended Contract, so three count. Total: three (3).
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (definition of Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly ten (10) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary from the last Season of her prior Contract.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary applicable to the Salary Cap Year in which the Offer is made.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Supermax Salary applicable to the Salary Cap Year in which the Offer is made.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' provision (D) states that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer shall be 'the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.' This rule applies regardless of years of service once a player qualifies under Sec. 5(b).
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2031 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) The Salary Cap for 2031 would have increased by more than 13% from the 2030 Salary Cap but for the cap, and the Shortfall equals the excess above the 13% increase multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) The Salary Cap for 2031 would have increased by more than 10% from the 2030 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii), and the Shortfall equals the excess above the 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams participating in the 2031 Season.
CC) Total Salaries for 2031 fall below the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams, and the Shortfall equals that deficit.
DD) The Salary Cap for 2031 would have decreased by more than 10% from the 2030 Salary Cap, and the Shortfall equals the deficit multiplied by the number of Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap 'would have increased by more than ten percent (10%)' of the prior Salary Cap Year's cap (the 13% threshold applies only to the 2027 Salary Cap Year). The Shortfall equals the amount in excess of the 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams participating that Season.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the out-of-network annual deductible per covered individual AND the out-of-network out-of-pocket threshold per person at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) $1,800 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after the individual pays $9,000 out-of-pocket.
BB) $2,500 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after the individual pays $8,000 out-of-pocket.
CC) $600 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after the individual pays $3,000 out-of-pocket.
DD) $2,500 annual deductible per individual; 100% coverage after the individual pays $20,000 out-of-pocket.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) provides that out-of-network services carry a '$2,500 annual deductible per covered individual' and coverage reaches 100% after 'the covered individual has paid $8,000 per person' in a calendar year. The $20,000 figure is the family out-of-pocket threshold, not the per-person threshold.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses six (6) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team imposes the maximum 2.5%-per-game fine for all six games. The WNBA then separately seeks to impose a suspension for those same six missed games. Which of the following CORRECTLY describes the interaction of the 20% aggregate cap with the suspension salary reduction?
AA) The suspension salary reduction is subject to the same 20% aggregate cap that applies to the fines, so the total combined reduction cannot exceed 20% of Base Salary.
BB) The 20% aggregate cap applies only to fines; the salary reduction resulting from the separately imposed suspension is not limited by the 20% cap and may be imposed in addition to the fines.
CC) Because fines have already been imposed, the WNBA is prohibited from also imposing a suspension for the same missed games.
DD) The 20% cap prevents any further action once fines reach 15% of Base Salary, so only a 5% further reduction via suspension is permissible.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that 'the 20% and 25% caps noted above shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension,' meaning the suspension salary reduction is entirely separate from and not limited by the aggregate fine cap. The Team or WNBA retains the right to impose a suspension for the same missed games notwithstanding the fine already imposed.
Q9Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2030 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.25% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) specifies that for the 2030 Salary Cap Year, the Designated 20% Share of SBR equals '30.5% of League Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2029 Salary Cap Year.' The 12.25% rate applies to 2029 and the 13% rate applies to 2031 and 2032.
Q10Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, a non-Developmental Player whose Base Salary is exactly $500,001 is entitled to Team-provided one-bedroom apartment housing in the home city of her WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a)(iii) limits Team-provided housing for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons to non-Developmental Players 'whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000.' A player earning $500,001 exceeds that threshold by one dollar and is therefore NOT entitled to Team-provided housing under this provision.
