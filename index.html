Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Lottery First Round Pick is currently in its FIRST Season and has NOT been extended. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes the status of the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection?
AA) The exception applies, so the contract does NOT count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection during the first Season.
BB) The exception does not apply because Lottery First Round Pick contracts are always counted against the seven-contract limit.
CC) The exception applies only if the team has fewer than seven protected contracts already on the roster.
DD) The exception does not apply until the second Season of the contract, so it counts against the limit in the first Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), any Rookie Scale Contract that contains Base Salary protection shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection, and this exception applies with respect to and during all four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract (not during any extended term). Since the contract is in its first Season and has not been extended, the exception applies.
Q2Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the first Regular Season covered by the Contract.
BB) The Base Salary for the first Season shall be fully protected immediately upon signing, with no condition precedent.
CC) The Base Salary for the first Season shall become fully protected only if the Team exercises the option for the fourth Season.
DD) The Base Salary for the first Season may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected in the Lottery, the Base Salary for the first Season shall be fully protected — there is no triggering condition for the first Season, unlike the second, third, and fourth Seasons which have specific conditions. This is a key distinction from non-Lottery First Round Picks.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly four (4) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) The Contract may contain a trade bonus because the player has at least three (3) Years of Service.
BB) The Contract may not contain a trade bonus because fewer than six (6) Years of Service are required.
CC) The Contract may contain a trade bonus only if the team agrees to waive the six-year threshold.
DD) The Contract may contain a trade bonus because four (4) Years of Service satisfies the minimum threshold.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. With only four (4) Years of Service, this player is ineligible to include a trade bonus.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays three (3) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and zero (0) Seasons under any Extension. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays two (2) Seasons under her Contract and four (4) Seasons under an Extension. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 9 Seasons total (3 + 6).
BB) 5 Seasons total (3 + 2), because only Seasons under the initial Contract — not Extension Seasons — count toward the limit.
CC) 7 Seasons total (3 + 4), because only Extension Seasons are excluded for Player A.
DD) 6 Seasons total (3 + 3), split evenly regardless of Extension terms.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), only the Seasons played under the Contract signed while a player was designated as a Core Player count toward the maximum — Extension Seasons are explicitly excluded. Player A contributes 3 Seasons and Player B contributes 2 Seasons (not the 4 Extension Seasons), for a total of 5.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly eleven (11) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's fourth-year Rookie Scale Base Salary.
BB) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) The Core Qualifying Offer amount equal to the Maximum Player Salary under Art. V, Sec. 8(c).
CBA Reference
Under Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Restricted Qualifying Offer for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d) must be the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. Years of Service beyond four (4) do not change this formula for a Restricted Qualifying Offer.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2027 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% but for the cap on annual increases; computed as the excess above the 10% threshold multiplied by the number of Teams.
BB) Triggered when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% from the 2026 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); computed as the excess above a 13% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2027 Season.
CC) Triggered whenever Total Salaries fall below the aggregate Salary Cap across all Teams; computed as the total shortfall across all Teams.
DD) Triggered when any individual Team's salary falls below the Minimum Team Salary; computed as 85% of the Salary Cap minus that Team's Team Salary.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2027 Salary Cap Year specifically, the Salary Cap Limit Shortfall is triggered only when the cap would have increased by more than thirteen percent (13%) from the 2026 Salary Cap but for the smoothing rule. The Shortfall equals the additional amount above the 13% ceiling multiplied by the total number of Teams in the 2027 Season.

Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses seven (7) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team fines her 2.5% of Base Salary per game for all seven games (totaling 17.5% of Base Salary). The WNBA then separately seeks to impose an additional fine of 4% of Base Salary for those same seven games. Which of the following is CORRECT?
AA) The WNBA's additional fine of 4% is permissible because the aggregate total (21.5%) would exceed the 20% cap only marginally, and a de minimis exception applies.
BB) The WNBA may not impose any additional fine for the same missed games because Art. XIV, Sec. 7 explicitly prohibits both the Team and the WNBA from imposing an additional fine for the same missed games once a fine has been imposed under that Section.
CC) The WNBA may impose the 4% additional fine, but it must be reduced so that the total does not exceed 20%, meaning the WNBA may impose only 2.5% instead.
DD) The WNBA may impose the 4% fine in full because the 20% cap applies only to Team fines, not WNBA fines.
CBA Reference
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, once the Team (or WNBA) imposes a fine under that Section for missed Regular Season games, neither the Team nor the WNBA may impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games. The WNBA's additional 4% fine is therefore impermissible regardless of whether the aggregate would exceed 20%.
Q9Art. VIII Sec. (c)T/F
True or False: Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by the same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), First Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by the percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap, while Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are instead adjusted by a flat 4% increase (not tied to Salary Cap movement). These are distinct adjustment mechanisms.
Q10Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii); Art. VII Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2026 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2025 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2025 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2025 Salary Cap Year, plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2025 Salary Cap Year.
CC) The 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' formula is not defined for the 2026 Salary Cap Year in Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) because the 2026 Salary Cap is set at a fixed $7,000,000 under Art. VII, Sec. 1(a).
DD) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year, plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2026 Salary Cap Year.
CBA Reference
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(a), the Salary Cap for the 2026 Salary Cap Year is set at a fixed $7,000,000 and does not use the Designated 20% Share of SBR formula. Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) only defines the formula beginning with the 2027 Salary Cap Year, so no such formula exists for 2026.
