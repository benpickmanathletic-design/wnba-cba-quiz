Q1Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected in the Lottery contains fully protected Base Salary. During which precise window does the Rookie Scale Contract exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection apply, and what event definitively ends it?
AA) The exception applies during any Season in which the Rookie Scale Contract is in force, including after an Extension is signed, and ends only upon Contract termination.
BB) The exception applies only during the four Seasons of the original Rookie Scale Contract term and ceases to apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Extension of that Contract.
CC) The exception applies during the first two Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract only and ends when the Team exercises its Fourth Year Option.
DD) The exception applies throughout the Rookie Scale Contract and any Extension, but only while the player's Base Salary remains at or below the applicable Rookie Scale Amount.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) expressly states the exception 'shall only apply with respect to and during the four Seasons of a Rookie Scale Contract and shall not apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.' The moment the Contract is extended, the exception terminates with respect to the extended term.
Q2Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), which of the following players is INELIGIBLE to have a trade bonus included in her Contract or Extension under the 2026 CBA?
AA) A player with exactly six (6) Years of Service at the time of signing a new Contract.
BB) A player with exactly seven (7) Years of Service at the time of signing an Extension.
CC) A player with exactly five (5) Years of Service at the time of signing a new Contract.
DD) A player with exactly eight (8) Years of Service at the time of signing a new Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) prohibits a trade bonus in any Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service. A player with exactly five (5) Years of Service has fewer than six and is therefore ineligible.
Q3Art. V Sec. 4(e)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a First Round Pick selected 'in the Lottery,' which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the FIRST Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the first Season is not protected at all; protection only attaches if waivers are not requested prior to the first day of the first Regular Season.
BB) The Base Salary for the first Season becomes fully protected only if the Team exercises the option for the fourth Season.
CC) The Base Salary for the first Season is fully protected immediately upon signing of the Contract, with no condition precedent.
DD) The Base Salary for the first Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by the Contract.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(i) provides that for a Lottery pick's Rookie Scale Contract, 'the Base Salary for the first Season of such Contract shall be fully protected' — this is an unconditional protection, unlike the non-Lottery First Round Pick rule which requires the Team not to have requested waivers before the first Regular Season.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays six (6) Seasons under the Contract signed while designated and zero (0) Seasons under any Extension. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays one (1) Season under her Contract and two (2) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 9 Seasons (6 + 1 + 2).
BB) 7 Seasons (6 + 1 only, because Extension Seasons do not count).
CC) 6 Seasons (only Player A's Seasons count; Player B's Extensions are excluded and her non-Extension Season is too few to trigger counting).
DD) 8 Seasons (6 + 1 + 1, counting only the first Extension Season for Player B).
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) states that only Seasons played 'under the Contract signed while such player was so designated (but not including Seasons covered by any Extension to such Contract)' are counted toward the maximum Core Player designation limit. Player A contributes 6 Seasons; Player B contributes 1 Season (not her 2 Extension Seasons), totaling 7.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly seven (7) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's Base Salary in the final Season of her prior Contract.
BB) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
CC) The Standard Maximum Salary for the applicable Salary Cap Year.
DD) The Minimum Annual Salary for a player with 1-3 Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer' clause (D) provides that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), the Restricted Qualifying Offer shall be 'the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.' Years of Service (7) satisfy the 4+ threshold under Sec. 5(b), making this the applicable floor.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(c)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(c), the Minimum Team Salary for a Salary Cap Year equals what percentage of the Salary Cap, and which of the following is the ONE recognized exception to the WNBA's obligation to cause a Team to make up any Minimum Team Salary shortfall at Season's end?
AA) 80%; a Team that trades away more than half its roster before the Season's midpoint is exempt.
BB) 85%; a Team that ceases operations prior to the conclusion of a WNBA Season is not required to meet the Minimum Team Salary obligation.
CC) 90%; a Team that is subject to an ongoing league investigation into financial irregularities is temporarily exempt.
DD) 85%; a Team that has fewer than ten (10) players on its roster due to injuries at Season's end is exempt.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(c)(i) sets the Minimum Team Salary at eighty-five percent (85%) of the Salary Cap. Sec. 1(c)(ii) provides the sole exception: 'any Team that ceases operations prior to the conclusion of a WNBA Season shall not be required to meet the Minimum Team Salary obligation.'
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the in-network annual deductible per covered individual AND the in-network out-of-pocket threshold at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) $600 annual deductible; 100% coverage after the covered individual has paid $5,000 out-of-pocket.
BB) $1,800 annual deductible (family rate applied to individual); 100% coverage after $9,000 out-of-pocket.
CC) $600 annual deductible; 100% coverage after the covered individual has paid $3,000 out-of-pocket in a calendar year.
DD) $2,500 annual deductible; 100% coverage after the covered individual has paid $8,000 out-of-pocket.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) specifies in-network services with 'a $600 annual deductible per covered individual' and 'coverage at eighty percent (80%) until the covered individual has paid $3,000 per person... in a calendar year, and one hundred percent (100%) thereafter.' The $2,500/$8,000 figures apply to out-of-network services.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 7T/F
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, if the WNBA or a Team imposes the maximum 2.5%-per-game fine on a player for missing Regular Season games AND the Team also wants to impose a suspension for those same missed Regular Season games, which of the following CORRECTLY describes the interaction of the 20% aggregate cap with the suspension salary reduction?
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that 'the 20% and 25% caps noted above shall not be applicable to any lost salary due to any such suspension,' meaning a Team retains the right to impose a suspension for the same missed games, and the resulting salary reduction is NOT subject to the 20% aggregate Regular Season fine cap. The statement that the cap DOES limit the suspension salary reduction is therefore False.
Q9Art. XII Sec. 1(a)(ii)4-choice
Under Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii), which of the following CORRECTLY states the formula for the 'Designated 20% Share of SBR' for the 2033 Salary Cap Year?
AA) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year plus 13% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
BB) 30.25% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year plus 12% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
CC) 30.5% of League Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year plus 12.5% of Team Revenue for the 2032 Salary Cap Year.
DD) The CBA does not specify a formula for the 2033 Salary Cap Year; the 2032 formula is the last expressly defined.
CBA Reference
Art. XII, Sec. 1(a)(ii) defines the Designated 20% Share of SBR through the 2031 and 2032 Salary Cap Years as the last expressly enumerated periods (using the immediately preceding year's revenues). The CBA term under Art. I covers through the 2032 Salary Cap Year (ending December 31, 2032), and no 2033 formula is provided in the Agreement.
Q10Art. XI Sec. 1(a)T/F
Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2031 and 2032 Seasons, a non-Developmental Player whose Base Salary is exactly $500,000 is entitled to a Team-provided one-bedroom apartment in the home city of her WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a) provides Team-provided housing for all non-Developmental Players unconditionally for the 2026, 2027, and 2028 Seasons, and for those with Base Salary 'less than or equal to $500,000' for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons only. No housing entitlement is specified for the 2031 or 2032 Seasons; therefore, a player at any salary level in 2031 or 2032 has no CBA-guaranteed Team-provided housing under this provision.
