Q1Art. V Sec. 4(e)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, which of the following CORRECTLY states the Base Salary protection rule for the SECOND Season of such Contract?
AA) The Base Salary for the second Season shall become fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract.
BB) The Base Salary for the second Season shall become fully protected only if the Team exercises the option for the fourth Season of such Contract.
CC) The Base Salary for the second Season shall be fully protected from the date of signing without any condition.
DD) The Base Salary for the second Season may be protected only for injury, not for lack of skill, if waivers are not requested prior to the first day of that Season.
CBA Reference
Under Art. V, Sec. 4(e)(iii), for a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Second Round Pick or Third Round Pick, the Base Salary for the second Season becomes fully protected if waivers are not requested on the player prior to the first day of the second Regular Season covered by such Contract. Notably, such contracts may not contain Base Salary protection of any kind for the first Season.
Q2Art. V Sec. 3(g)(vi)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi), a player has exactly six (6) Years of Service at the time she signs a new Contract. Which of the following CORRECTLY states her eligibility to include a trade bonus in that Contract?
AA) She is ineligible because the threshold is seven (7) Years of Service.
BB) She is eligible because she has at least six (6) Years of Service, which satisfies the minimum requirement.
CC) She is eligible only if the Contract provides for a Base Salary above the Standard Maximum Salary.
DD) She is ineligible because trade bonuses may only be included in Contracts of players with eight (8) or more Years of Service.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(g)(vi) states that no Contract or Extension entered into by a player who, at the time of signing, has fewer than six (6) Years of Service may contain a trade bonus. A player with exactly six (6) Years of Service meets the minimum threshold and is therefore eligible.
Q3Art. V Sec. 3(b)(iii)4-choice
Under Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii), a Rookie Scale Contract signed by a Third Round Pick that has NOT been extended contains conditional Base Salary protection. Which of the following MOST ACCURATELY describes whether such a Contract benefits from the exception to the per-team limit of seven (7) Player Contracts with Base Salary protection AND when that exception ends?
AA) The exception applies during all four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract; it ends upon the expiration of the fourth Season.
BB) The exception applies only during the first two Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract, ceasing at the start of the third Season.
CC) The exception applies with respect to and during the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract; it does not apply with respect to or during the extended term of any Rookie Scale Contract that has been extended.
DD) No exception applies to Third Round Picks because their Rookie Scale Contracts do not contain any Base Salary protection.
CBA Reference
Art. V, Sec. 3(b)(iii) expressly provides that a Rookie Scale Contract containing Base Salary protection (including conditional protection) shall not count against the per-team limit of seven (7) Contracts with Base Salary protection, but clarifies this exception only applies during the four Seasons of the Rookie Scale Contract itself and ceases upon extension. A Third Round Pick's Rookie Scale Contract may contain conditional protection (second Season and beyond) and qualifies for this exception.
Q4Art. VI Sec. 7(a)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 7(a), a Team designates Player A as a Core Player. She plays one (1) Season under the Contract signed while designated and five (5) Seasons under an Extension of that Contract. The Team later designates Player B as a Core Player and she plays three (3) Seasons under her Contract (no extension). How many total Seasons count toward the Team's maximum Core Player designation limit?
AA) 9 Seasons total (6 for Player A, 3 for Player B)
BB) 4 Seasons total (1 for Player A, 3 for Player B)
CC) 8 Seasons total (5 for Player A, 3 for Player B)
DD) 6 Seasons total (3 for Player A, 3 for Player B)
CBA Reference
Art. VI, Sec. 7(a) specifies that only the Seasons played under the Contract signed while the player was designated as a Core Player count toward the limit—Seasons under any Extension to that Contract are expressly excluded. Player A contributes only 1 Season (the one Season under the original Contract, not the 5 under the Extension), and Player B contributes 3 Seasons, for a total of 4.
Q5Art. VI Sec. 5(b); Art. I (Qualifying Offer definition)4-choice
Under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b) and Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' a Veteran Free Agent with exactly six (6) Years of Service receives a Restricted Qualifying Offer from her Prior Team. Which of the following CORRECTLY states the salary floor for that Restricted Qualifying Offer?
AA) 105% of the player's fourth year Rookie Scale Contract Base Salary.
BB) The Maximum Player Salary pursuant to Art. V, Sec. 8(c).
CC) The greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract.
DD) A flat amount of $270,000 regardless of prior Contract terms.
CBA Reference
Art. I's definition of 'Qualifying Offer,' subsection (D), states that for a player subject to a right of first refusal under Art. VI, Sec. 5(b)-(d), a Restricted Qualifying Offer must equal the greater of the applicable Minimum Player Salary or the Base Salary contained in the last Season of the player's prior Contract. A Veteran Free Agent with six (6) Years of Service qualifying under Sec. 5(b) falls squarely within this rule.
Q6Art. VII Sec. 1(b)(i)4-choice
Under Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i), for the 2028 Salary Cap Year, a 'Salary Cap Limit Shortfall' is triggered only when which specific condition is met, and how is the Shortfall amount computed?
AA) The Salary Cap would have increased by more than 13% from the prior year's cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the excess above a 13% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams.
BB) The Salary Cap would have increased by more than 10% from the 2027 Salary Cap but for the operation of Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the excess above a 10% increase multiplied by the total number of Teams.
CC) Total Salaries for the 2028 Salary Cap Year are less than the Salary Cap multiplied by the number of Teams; the Shortfall equals that difference.
DD) The Salary Cap would have decreased by more than 10% but for Sec. 1(a)(ii); the Shortfall equals the amount of that excess decrease multiplied by total Teams.
CBA Reference
Art. VII, Sec. 1(b)(i) provides that a Salary Cap Limit Shortfall arises when the Salary Cap would have increased by more than the applicable cap rate—10% for Salary Cap Years following 2027 (the 13% threshold only applies to 2027)—but for Sec. 1(a)(ii). For 2028, the applicable threshold is 10% over the 2027 cap, and the Shortfall equals the additional amount above that 10% ceiling multiplied by the number of Teams.
Q7Art. X Sec. 1(a)(i)4-choice
Under Art. X, Sec. 1(a), for a non-Toronto Tempo player, which of the following CORRECTLY states the in-network annual deductible per covered individual, the in-network coverage percentage after the deductible is met but before the out-of-pocket threshold is reached, AND the in-network out-of-pocket threshold per person at which coverage reaches 100%?
AA) $600 deductible; 80% coverage until $3,000 per person out-of-pocket; 100% thereafter.
BB) $600 deductible; 70% coverage until $3,000 per person out-of-pocket; 100% thereafter.
CC) $1,800 deductible; 80% coverage until $9,000 per family out-of-pocket; 100% thereafter.
DD) $2,500 deductible; 80% coverage until $8,000 per person out-of-pocket; 100% thereafter.
CBA Reference
Art. X, Sec. 1(a)(i) provides that for non-Toronto Tempo players, in-network services carry a $600 annual deductible per covered individual, coverage at 80% until the covered individual has paid $3,000 per person in a calendar year, and 100% coverage thereafter, with no annual or lifetime maximum benefit.
Q8Art. XIV Sec. 74-choice
Under Art. XIV, Sec. 7, a player misses eight (8) Regular Season games without authorization. The Team imposes the maximum 2.5%-per-game fine for all eight games (totaling 20% of Base Salary). The Team then seeks to impose an additional fine (not a suspension) for those same eight missed games. Which of the following is CORRECT?
AA) The Team may impose an additional fine because the 20% aggregate cap applies only to the WNBA, not to Teams.
BB) The Team may not impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games; having already fined her at the 2.5%-per-game rate, the 20% aggregate cap is reached and no further fine is permitted for those games.
CC) The Team may impose an additional fine up to 5% of Base Salary for the same games because the 20% cap does not apply once the WNBA has acted.
DD) The Team may impose an additional fine of any amount because the 20% cap resets each month.
CBA Reference
Art. XIV, Sec. 7 expressly states that in no event may aggregate fines exceed 20% of Base Salary for missing Regular Season games in any Season, and further clarifies that if the WNBA or a Team imposes a fine under Sec. 7 for missed Regular Season games, neither the Team nor the WNBA shall be permitted to impose an additional fine on the player for the same missed games. The Team has already reached the 20% ceiling and cannot fine further for those same games.
Q9Art. XI Sec. 1(a)(iii)T/F
True or False: Under Art. XI, Sec. 1(a), for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, a non-Developmental Player whose Base Salary is exactly $500,001 (one dollar above $500,000) is NOT entitled to a Team-provided one-bedroom apartment in the home city of her WNBA Team.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. XI, Sec. 1(a)(iii) provides that for the 2029 and 2030 Seasons, Team-provided housing is available only to non-Developmental Players whose Base Salary is less than or equal to $500,000. A player earning $500,001 exceeds that threshold and therefore is not entitled to Team-provided housing under this provision.
Q10Art. VIII Sec. (c)T/F
True or False: Under Art. VIII, Sec. (c), for Rookie Scale Contracts commencing with Seasons after 2026, the Rookie Scale Amounts applicable to Second Round Picks and Third Round Picks are adjusted by the same percentage increase (or decrease) in the Salary Cap that governs First Round Pick adjustments rather than by a fixed 4% increase.
ATrue
BFalse
CBA Reference
Art. VIII, Sec. (c) establishes two different adjustment mechanisms: First Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by the percentage increase or decrease in the Salary Cap, while Second Round Pick and Third Round Pick Rookie Scale Amounts are adjusted by a fixed 4% increase per Season, not by the Salary Cap percentage change.
